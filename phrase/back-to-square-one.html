back to square one

IdiomLevel B1CasualProblems & solutions

You have to start again from the beginning because your first try did not work.

The client hated the design, so we're back to square one.

Tip: Use it with 'be' or 'be right': 'we're back to square one'. It often follows bad news, and it sounds natural in work, study and everyday plans. Don't use it for a small step backward.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid the landlord say yes to the new lease?
DiegoNo. He found another tenant who'll pay more, so he's ending ours in March.
AmiraOh no. After all that paperwork?
DiegoI know. I've been looking at flats for weeks, and now we're back to square one.
AmiraCome on, let's go through the listings together tonight.

2. Fill the gap

3. Choose the best reply

Kofi: I spent all weekend on my essay, and then my laptop crashed without saving it.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when a plan you made didn't work out and you had to start again.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I planned a trip with friends, but the hotel cancelled our booking two days before we left. We had already arranged flights and a schedule, so we were back to square one. In the end we found a different place, and it turned out even better.

Answer key: back to square one

  1. 1. C. They have to start the search again because their plan failed.
  2. 2. back to square one
  3. 3. C. That's awful. So you're back to square one?
  4. 4. Example: Last year I planned a trip with friends, but the hotel cancelled our booking two days before we left. We had already arranged flights and a schedule, so we were back to square one. In the end we found a different place, and it turned out even better.
