spick and span

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

Very clean and tidy.

She made the whole house spick and span before the guests arrived.

Tip: Use it after a verb like 'be', 'keep' or 'leave' to describe a place or thing that is spotless and orderly; it's about cleanliness, not about speed.

Hear it in conversation

KenHave you seen Olena's new flat? I dropped off her keys yesterday.
AmiraNot yet. What's it like?
KenHonestly, I was shocked. There wasn't a single dish in the sink, and the floors were spick and span.
AmiraThat's so like her. My place looks like a storm hit it by Tuesday.
KenMine too. I think I'll ask her for tips.

Show an example answer

I'm quite a tidy person, so I clean a little every day. But before friends come over, I do a proper clean and make the whole place spick and span. It just makes me feel more relaxed when people are around.

Answer key: spick and span

  1. 1. B. Very clean and tidy
  2. 2. spick and span
  3. 3. C. I spent Sunday cleaning it, so it's spick and span now.
  4. 4. Example: I'm quite a tidy person, so I clean a little every day. But before friends come over, I do a proper clean and make the whole place spick and span. It just makes me feel more relaxed when people are around.
