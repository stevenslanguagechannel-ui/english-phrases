cut to the chase

IdiomLevel B2NeutralWork & study

To stop the small talk and get to the main point.

I'm busy, so please cut to the chase.

Tip: Say 'cut to the chase' (often after 'let's' or 'I'll') when you want to skip the background and give or ask for the key point; it can sound a little blunt if you tell a person to do it.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHi Kofi, thanks for calling back. How was your weekend? Did you get to the coast in the end?
KofiWe did, it was lovely. The traffic was awful on the way home, though, and the hotel...
OlenaSorry to interrupt! I have a meeting in five minutes, so I'll cut to the chase. Can you cover my Thursday shift?
KofiAh, okay. Thursday afternoon? Yes, I think I can do that.
OlenaBrilliant, thank you. I'll tell you everything about it later.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'cut to the chase' mean?

2. Fill the gap

We only have ten minutes left, so could you and tell us what the client actually decided?

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: Well, the project started last spring, and before that we had a few different suppliers, and, um, the budget was a bit different then...

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when someone took too long to explain something to you. What did you do?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month my landlord rang me about a repair, and he spent ten minutes talking about the building's history. In the end I politely asked him to cut to the chase, and he told me the plumber was coming on Friday. It was a bit awkward, but I saved a lot of time.

Answer key: cut to the chase

  1. 1. C. Go straight to the main point without more chat
  2. 2. cut to the chase
  3. 3. B. Sorry, Amira, but could you cut to the chase? Are we going over budget?
  4. 4. Example: Last month my landlord rang me about a repair, and he spent ten minutes talking about the building's history. In the end I politely asked him to cut to the chase, and he told me the plumber was coming on Friday. It was a bit awkward, but I saved a lot of time.
