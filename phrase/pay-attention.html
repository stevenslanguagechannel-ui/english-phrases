pay attention

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralWork & study

To listen and watch carefully.

Please pay attention. This part will be on the test.

Tip: Say 'pay attention to' + noun (pay attention to the details), or use it alone as a command; don't say 'give attention' or 'pay an attention'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow was the training session this morning?
KofiHonestly, I missed half of it. I was checking emails.
OlenaOh no. Isn't the new booking system starting on Monday?
KofiYes, that's the problem. I should have paid attention to the steps.
OlenaI took notes. Do you want to look at them over lunch?
KofiYes, please. You're a lifesaver.

4. Your turn: speak

Describe a time when it was hard to concentrate. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last week I tried to study in a busy cafe, and I couldn't pay attention to my book at all. People kept talking loudly and the music was too loud. In the end I went home, and I got much more done there.

Answer key: pay attention

  1. 1. D. Listened and watched carefully
  2. 2. pay attention
  3. 3. B. You really should pay attention to the announcements.
  4. 4. Example: Last week I tried to study in a busy cafe, and I couldn't pay attention to my book at all. People kept talking loudly and the music was too loud. In the end I went home, and I got much more done there.
