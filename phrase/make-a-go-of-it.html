make a go of it

IdiomLevel B2NeutralSuccess & progress

To try hard to make something you have started become successful.

He loves painting, so he's going to make a go of it as an artist.

Tip: Use it as 'make a go of it' (or 'make a go of + noun') after you have already started something, like a business, a move or a relationship; it's often used with 'decide to' or 'want to'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaI heard you've been selling your candles at the weekend market.
AmiraYes, it started as a hobby, but people keep coming back for more.
OlenaSo are you going to keep it small?
AmiraNo, I've decided to make a go of it. I'm cutting my hours at the office to give it a proper chance.
OlenaThat's brave. Let me know if you need help with the website.
