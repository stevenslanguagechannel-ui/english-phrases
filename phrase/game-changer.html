game changer

IdiomLevel B2CasualSuccess & progress

Something that completely changes a situation, usually for the better.

The new bus line is a game changer for my morning commute.

Tip: Say 'a game changer' or 'a real game changer' for something that makes a big difference; it's often followed by 'for' + the person or situation affected, and it's usually positive.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraHow's the new job going? Are you still driving for an hour each way?
DiegoNo, that's the best part. They let us work from home three days a week now.
AmiraThree days? That's a big change.
DiegoHonestly, it's a game changer. I have time to cook and go running again.
AmiraI'm jealous. My office still wants us there every day.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

For me, a good pair of noise-cancelling headphones was a game changer. I used to struggle to concentrate in my busy flat, but now I can study for hours without getting distracted. It has honestly made my days much more productive.
