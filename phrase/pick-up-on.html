pick up on

Phrasal verbLevel B2NeutralPeople & relationships

To notice something small or hidden, like a feeling or a change.

She picked up on his nervous tone right away.

Tip: Use 'pick up on + noun' (pick up on the tension, a change, a hint); it often describes noticing something that nobody said out loud.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid you talk to Kofi after the meeting? He seemed really quiet.
DiegoYeah. I think he's worried about his project, but he didn't say it.
OlenaHow do you know, then?
DiegoI picked up on the way he kept checking his phone and changing the subject.
OlenaGood point. Maybe I'll invite him for lunch tomorrow.
DiegoDo that. I think he'd really appreciate it.
