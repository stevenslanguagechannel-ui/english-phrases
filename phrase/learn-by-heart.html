learn by heart

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralWork & study

To remember something so well that you can say it without looking.

We had to learn the poem by heart for tomorrow.

Tip: Use it as 'learn (something) by heart': the thing you memorise goes between 'learn' and 'by heart', and it works for words, lists, speeches and numbers.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you nervous about the presentation on Friday?
KofiA bit. I've written the whole speech, but I don't want to read from my notes.
OlenaSo what's the plan?
KofiI'm going to learn the opening and the closing by heart, and just use bullet points for the middle.
OlenaGood idea. Practise it in the car on the way to work.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'learn the opening and the closing by heart' mean?

2. Fill the gap

My grandmother can still say every song from her school days, because she had to when she was a child.

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: I have a driving theory exam next week, and there are so many road signs to remember!

4. Your turn: speak

Is there anything you had to memorise at school or at work? How did you do it, and was it useful?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

At school we had to learn long lists of irregular verbs by heart, and I wrote them on small cards and read them on the train. It was boring, but I still remember most of them today. I think learning some things by heart is useful, as long as you also practise using them in real conversations.

Answer key: learn by heart

  1. 1. A. Memorise them so well that he can say them without his notes
  2. 2. learn them by heart
  3. 3. B. Just learn them by heart, and test yourself with flashcards on the bus.
  4. 4. Example: At school we had to learn long lists of irregular verbs by heart, and I wrote them on small cards and read them on the train. It was boring, but I still remember most of them today. I think learning some things by heart is useful, as long as you also practise using them in real conversations.
