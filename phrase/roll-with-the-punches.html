roll with the punches

IdiomLevel B2CasualProblems & solutions

To stay calm and adapt when things go wrong or change suddenly.

Our flight was cancelled, but we rolled with the punches and took a train.

Tip: Use it as a verb phrase about people ("learn to roll with the punches", "she rolls with the punches"); it describes a flexible attitude to setbacks, not a single event.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid the landlord really move the viewing to tomorrow morning?
KofiYes, and the plumber can only come at the same time.
AmiraUgh. I had everything planned. Aren't you stressed?
KofiA bit, but you have to roll with the punches in a rented flat.
AmiraTrue. I'll ask my sister to meet the plumber instead.
KofiGood idea. Problem solved.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when your plans suddenly changed. How did you deal with it?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last summer, my train was delayed and I missed an important family dinner. I was disappointed at first, but I decided to roll with the punches and joined everyone by video call instead. It turned out to be quite fun.

Answer key: roll with the punches

  1. 1. C. Accept changes calmly and adjust to them
  2. 2. rolled with the punches
  3. 3. A. Annoying, but we'll just have to roll with the punches and adapt.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer, my train was delayed and I missed an important family dinner. I was disappointed at first, but I decided to roll with the punches and joined everyone by video call instead. It turned out to be quite fun.
