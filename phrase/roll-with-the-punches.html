roll with the punches

IdiomLevel B2

To stay calm and adapt when things go wrong or change suddenly.

Our flight was cancelled, but we rolled with the punches and took a train.

Roll with the punches means to adapt calmly when problems appear. It is used for plans, jobs and travel.
