hit or miss

IdiomLevel B2

Sometimes good and sometimes bad, so you can't predict the result.

The bus is hit or miss in the morning: sometimes it's early, sometimes it never comes.

Use 'hit or miss' for things that are sometimes good and sometimes bad. You can say 'It's hit or miss'.
