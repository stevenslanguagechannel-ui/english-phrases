hit or miss

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

Sometimes good and sometimes bad, so you can't predict the result.

The bus is hit or miss in the morning: sometimes it's early, sometimes it never comes.

Tip: Use it after 'be' or 'is' ("The signal here is hit or miss") to describe things that vary from time to time, like service, weather, quality or advice; it's not usually used for people's character.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you going to try that new sandwich place near the station?
KofiI went twice last week. The first time it was amazing.
OlenaAnd the second time?
KofiThe bread was dry and they forgot my drink. It's hit or miss, I think.
OlenaHmm. Maybe I'll just bring lunch from home on Friday.

Show an example answer

The Wi-Fi on my train is really hit or miss. Some mornings I can join a video call without any problems, and other days I can't even load an email. Because of that, I never plan important work for the journey.

Answer key: hit or miss

  1. 1. D. The quality changes and you can't be sure what you'll get
  2. 2. hit or miss
  3. 3. A. Honestly, it's hit or miss. Sometimes everything's fresh, sometimes half the order is wrong.
  4. 4. Example: The Wi-Fi on my train is really hit or miss. Some mornings I can join a video call without any problems, and other days I can't even load an email. Because of that, I never plan important work for the journey.
