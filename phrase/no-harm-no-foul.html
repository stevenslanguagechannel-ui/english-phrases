no harm, no foul

IdiomLevel B2CasualFeelings & reactions

Nothing bad happened, so there is no need to worry or blame anyone.

You were five minutes late, but we hadn't started. No harm, no foul.

Tip: Say it after a small mistake or accident that had no real consequences; it's usually said by the person who was affected, to let the other person relax.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraKen, I'm so sorry. I think I sat on your sandwich in the staff room.
KenOh, the one in the blue bag? I already ate it at ten o'clock.
AmiraReally? Then I just squashed an empty bag. I felt terrible for a minute.
KenDon't worry about it. No harm, no foul.
AmiraThanks. I'll buy you a coffee anyway.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "no harm, no foul" mean?

2. Fill the gap

The parcel arrived a day late, but the gift was still in time for her birthday, so .

3. Choose the best reply

Olena: Sorry, I took your charger by mistake, but I only used it for ten minutes and it's back on your desk.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you made a small mistake but it didn't cause any problems.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I sent a meeting invitation to the wrong time, but I noticed it within a few minutes and fixed it before anyone saw it. My manager just laughed and said, "No harm, no foul." I was relieved, but I now check the time twice before I send anything.

Answer key: no harm, no foul

  1. 1. C. Nothing bad came from it, so there's no reason to be upset.
  2. 2. no harm, no foul
  3. 3. C. It's fine, no harm, no foul.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I sent a meeting invitation to the wrong time, but I noticed it within a few minutes and fixed it before anyone saw it. My manager just laughed and said, "No harm, no foul." I was relieved, but I now check the time twice before I send anything.
