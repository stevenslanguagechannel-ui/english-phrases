no harm, no foul

IdiomLevel B2

Nothing bad happened, so there is no need to worry or blame anyone.

You were five minutes late, but we hadn't started. No harm, no foul.

Say 'no harm, no foul' when a mistake did not cause any problem. It comes from sports, but we use it in daily life.
