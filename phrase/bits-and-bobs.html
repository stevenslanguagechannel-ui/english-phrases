bits and bobs

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

Small, random things of different kinds.

My desk drawer is full of bits and bobs, like old keys and cables.

Tip: Say 'bits and bobs' as a plural noun phrase, often after 'some' or 'a few', or to finish a list of small items; it's more common in British English but widely understood.

Hear it in conversation

KofiAre you all packed for the move?
AmiraNearly. The furniture is easy, but I still have a few bits and bobs to deal with.
KofiLike what?
AmiraPhone chargers, spare buttons, a tape measure. Nothing big, but I can't find a box for it all.
KofiI've got an empty shoebox you can use.
AmiraPerfect, that's exactly the size I need.

Show an example answer

I'm always collecting bits and bobs for my desk, like little notebooks, pens and sticky notes. None of them are very useful on their own, but I like having them nearby. Every few months I have to clear them out because they take over the whole drawer.

Answer key: bits and bobs

  1. 1. C. Small, random things of different kinds
  2. 2. bits and bobs
  3. 3. B. Oh, just bits and bobs I might need later.
