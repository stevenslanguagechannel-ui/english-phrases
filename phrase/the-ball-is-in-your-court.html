the ball is in your court

IdiomLevel B2NeutralWork & study

It is now your turn to decide or act.

I sent you my offer, so the ball is in your court now.

Tip: Use it as a clause, usually 'the ball is in your/his/her/their court (now)', after someone has done their part and the next decision or action belongs to someone else; don't use it for a literal ball game or to blame someone.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid you hear back from the landlord about the flat?
KofiNot yet. I sent him the signed contract and my deposit details on Monday.
OlenaSo you've done everything you can.
KofiYes, the ball is in his court now. I'll just wait for his reply.
OlenaGive it until Friday, then send a polite reminder.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I applied for a short course abroad, and I sent all the documents a month early. After that, the ball was in the university's court, so I just had to wait. It was stressful, but I was happy when they finally said yes.

Answer key: the ball is in your court

  1. 1. D. The landlord now has to make the next decision or take the next step.
  2. 2. ball is in their court
  3. 3. B. Nothing for you to do now. The ball is in her court.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I applied for a short course abroad, and I sent all the documents a month early. After that, the ball was in the university's court, so I just had to wait. It was stressful, but I was happy when they finally said yes.
