have the travel bug

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

If you have the travel bug, you have a strong wish to travel a lot.

Ever since my trip to Italy, I've had the travel bug.

Tip: Use 'have the travel bug' (or 'get the travel bug') for a strong, ongoing urge to travel; it's friendly and positive, and you can say 'I've caught the travel bug' to describe when it started.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaIs that a new map on your wall, Kofi?
KofiYes, I'm marking every place I want to visit. I've booked flights to Lisbon for March.
OlenaAgain? You only got back from Vietnam last month.
KofiI know, I know. I think I have the travel bug. I check flight prices every evening.
OlenaWell, save a seat for me. I'd love to see Lisbon.

4. Your turn: speak

Do you enjoy travelling? Tell me about a time you wanted to go somewhere new.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Yes, I love it. A few years ago I spent a weekend in a small coastal town, and I've had the travel bug ever since. Now I try to plan at least one new trip every year, even if it's only a short one.

Answer key: have the travel bug

  1. 1. D. Kofi really wants to travel a lot and keeps planning new trips
  2. 2. had the travel bug
  3. 3. C. Haha, I can't help it. I've really got the travel bug.
