team up

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralWork & study

To join someone and work together to do something.

Let's team up and cook dinner together tonight.

Tip: Use 'team up with someone' or 'team up to do something'; it often describes a choice to work together, so it's common in plans, projects and games.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHave you started the group presentation yet? I'm still stuck on the slides.
KofiNot really. I've got the research done, but my design skills are terrible.
OlenaWell, I'm good at design but I hate searching for data.
KofiThen why don't we team up? You make the slides, and I'll send you everything I found.
OlenaPerfect. Send me your notes tonight and I'll start tomorrow morning.
