swap out

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralProblems & solutions

To swap something out is to remove it and put a different one in its place.

Can you swap out the old batteries for new ones?

Tip: Use it as 'swap out A for B' or 'swap A out for B'; with a pronoun the object must go in the middle ('swap it out', not 'swap out it').

Hear it in conversation

AmiraMy laptop keeps overheating when I'm on video calls.
KenHave you checked the fan? Mine was clogged with dust.
AmiraI cleaned it, but it didn't help. The technician said the battery is the problem.
KenThen you should swap it out. A new battery isn't expensive.
AmiraTrue. I'll book it in for Saturday.

  2. 2. swap it out
