my mouth is watering

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

I want to eat something very much because it smells or looks delicious.

That pizza smells so good that my mouth is watering.

Tip: Use it with a subject like 'my mouth' or 'her mouth', usually in the present continuous; people often say it when they see, smell or even just talk about food they want.

Hear it in conversation

KofiAre you coming to the market on Saturday?
AmiraMaybe. What's there?
KofiThere's a stall that sells fresh bread and hot spicy noodles. You can smell it from the car park.
AmiraStop it, my mouth is watering already!
KofiThen come early. The queue gets long by eleven.
AmiraFine, I'll meet you at ten.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a food or a dish that always makes you hungry when you think about it.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

My grandmother's chicken soup is the one. When I walk past a restaurant that smells like it, my mouth is watering straight away. I usually end up going home and trying to cook it myself.

Answer key: my mouth is watering

  1. 1. B. I really want to eat that food because it sounds delicious.
  2. 2. mouth is watering
  3. 3. B. Wow, it smells amazing. My mouth is watering!
  4. 4. Example: My grandmother's chicken soup is the one. When I walk past a restaurant that smells like it, my mouth is watering straight away. I usually end up going home and trying to cook it myself.
