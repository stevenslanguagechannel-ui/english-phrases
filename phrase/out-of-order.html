out of order

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

Broken and not working.

We took the stairs because the elevator was out of order.

Tip: Use it for machines, equipment and public facilities, as in 'The machine is out of order', and don't use it for people or for things that are only slow or empty.

Hear it in conversation

KenDid you manage to print the boarding passes?
AmiraNot yet. The printer in the hotel lobby is out of order.
KenOh no. Is there another one anywhere?
AmiraThe receptionist said there's a copy shop around the corner. I'll walk over now.
KenGreat. I'll finish packing, then we can check out.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when something you needed to use in a public place wasn't working.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I went to the gym before work, but the only free locker room shower was out of order. I had to wash quickly in the sink and then I was nearly late for a meeting. Now I always check the gym's website for any problems first.

Answer key: out of order

  1. 1. A. Broken and not working
  2. 2. out of order
  3. 3. B. Sorry, it's out of order. There are paper towels by the sink.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I went to the gym before work, but the only free locker room shower was out of order. I had to wash quickly in the sink and then I was nearly late for a meeting. Now I always check the gym's website for any problems first.
