chances are

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralOpinions & honesty

It is likely that something is true or will happen.

Chances are it will rain this afternoon, so take an umbrella.

Tip: Use it at the start of a sentence, followed by a full clause ("Chances are he's already left"); it's a prediction, so don't use it for things you know for certain.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid Kofi reply about Saturday? I need to know how many chairs to borrow.
MeiNot yet. He's been working late all week.
OlenaSo is he coming or not?
MeiChances are he'll come. He never misses a birthday.
OlenaOkay, I'll plan for ten people, then.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "chances are he'll come" mean?

2. Fill the gap

I've left three messages for the plumber, so he's busy with another job.

3. Choose the best reply

Diego: Do you think Amira will like the gift I bought her?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you had to guess what someone would do. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year my friend didn't answer my calls before a trip, so I had to guess whether he'd be at the station. Chances are he was still packing, I thought, because he always leaves things to the last minute. I was right, and he arrived five minutes before the train.

Answer key: chances are

  1. 1. C. He will probably come.
  2. 2. chances are
  3. 3. B. Chances are she will. It's exactly her style.
  4. 4. Example: Last year my friend didn't answer my calls before a trip, so I had to guess whether he'd be at the station. Chances are he was still packing, I thought, because he always leaves things to the last minute. I was right, and he arrived five minutes before the train.
