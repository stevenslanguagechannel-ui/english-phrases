pay off

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To finish paying all the money you owe for something.

I finally paid off my car loan last month.

Tip: Use it with the debt as the object: 'pay off a loan/the mortgage/my card' or 'pay it off'. Don't confuse it with the different meaning 'pay off' = 'bring a good result', as in 'The risk paid off'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you still sending money to the bank every month for your laptop?
KofiNot anymore. I paid it off in October.
OlenaNice! So what are you going to do with the extra money?
KofiSave for a trip, I think. I'd like to visit my cousin in Lisbon.
OlenaGood plan. You deserve a holiday.

4. Your turn: speak

Have you ever saved money for something expensive? Tell me about it.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Yes, I bought a second-hand piano a few years ago. I paid a small amount every month, and I paid it off after about two years. It felt great to finally own it completely.

Answer key: pay off

  1. 1. D. He finished paying all the money he owed for it.
  2. 2. pay off
  3. 3. A. That's great! When did you pay it off?
