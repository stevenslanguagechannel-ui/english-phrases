run out of juice

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To have no battery power left.

My phone ran out of juice on the bus, so I couldn't call you.

Tip: Use it for phones, laptops and other devices ("my laptop's run out of juice"), and add "of" plus "juice"; it's informal, so save it for friendly conversation rather than formal writing.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraKen, I tried calling you three times last night. Where were you?
KenSorry! I was filming the sunset at the beach, and my phone ran out of juice.
AmiraAh, that explains it. I thought you were ignoring me.
KenNever! I only found my charger at midnight.
AmiraNext time take a power bank with you.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when your phone or another device stopped working at a bad moment. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last summer I was travelling to a new city, and my phone ran out of juice just as I was looking for my hostel. I didn't have a power bank, so I had to ask a shop assistant for directions and charge my phone in a café. Now I always carry a charger when I travel.

Answer key: run out of juice

  1. 1. D. The battery had no power left
  2. 2. run out of juice
  3. 3. A. Sorry, my laptop ran out of juice and I couldn't find the charger.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer I was travelling to a new city, and my phone ran out of juice just as I was looking for my hostel. I didn't have a power bank, so I had to ask a shop assistant for directions and charge my phone in a café. Now I always carry a charger when I travel.
