keep up appearances

IdiomLevel B2NeutralPeople & relationships

To act as if everything is fine or normal, even when it isn't.

The kitchen was a mess, but she kept up appearances and smiled at the guests.

Tip: Use it as a verb phrase, often with 'to' or after 'trying': 'We kept up appearances for the kids'; it often suggests hiding a problem, so don't use it for simply being polite.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraHow was dinner with Ken's parents on Saturday?
OlenaHonestly, awful. Ken and I had argued all afternoon.
AmiraOh no. Did they notice?
OlenaI don't think so. We kept up appearances until dessert, then we both went quiet.
AmiraThat sounds exhausting. Have you two talked since?
OlenaYes, last night. We're fine now, just tired.

4. Your turn: speak

Have you ever had to act as if everything was fine when it wasn't? What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Yes, once at work I was very stressed about a deadline, but I kept up appearances in meetings so my team wouldn't worry. It was hard, because I wanted to be honest with them. In the end I told my manager the truth, and I felt much better.

Answer key: keep up appearances

  1. 1. A. Acted as if everything was fine, even though it wasn't
  2. 2. kept up appearances
  3. 3. B. Yes, they were probably just keeping up appearances.
  4. 4. Example: Yes, once at work I was very stressed about a deadline, but I kept up appearances in meetings so my team wouldn't worry. It was hard, because I wanted to be honest with them. In the end I told my manager the truth, and I felt much better.
