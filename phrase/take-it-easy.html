take it easy

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To rest and not do too much.

The doctor told me to take it easy this week.

Tip: Often used as a friendly instruction or advice ("Take it easy!"), and also as a goodbye; it can mean "relax" or "don't overdo it", and the phrase stays fixed.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraYou look exhausted, Kofi. Did you go to the gym again this morning?
KofiYeah, and then I played football with my cousins. My legs feel like jelly.
AmiraMaybe you should take it easy this weekend.
KofiI know, but I'm training for a 10K next month.
AmiraA short, gentle run is fine, but don't push yourself too hard before the race.
KofiOkay, okay. I'll stay home tomorrow and just stretch.

4. Your turn: speak

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I had a huge deadline at work and I was completely worn out. Afterwards, I took a few days off and decided to take it easy, so I just slept, read, and went for short walks. It really helped me recover.

Answer key: take it easy

  1. 1. B. Rest and avoid doing too much
  2. 2. take it easy
  3. 3. B. You should take it easy for a few days and look after yourself.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I had a huge deadline at work and I was completely worn out. Afterwards, I took a few days off and decided to take it easy, so I just slept, read, and went for short walks. It really helped me recover.
