just saying

ExpressionLevel B2

A light phrase you add after a comment to say you don't want to argue or cause trouble.

That jacket looks a bit small. Just saying!

Add 'just saying' after a comment to show you don't mean to criticise or argue. Keep a light tone.

Your turn
