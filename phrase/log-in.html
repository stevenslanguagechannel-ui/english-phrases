log in

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To log in is to enter your name and password to use a website or app.

I can't log in. I forgot my password.

Tip: Say 'log in' (two words) for the action, 'log in to' + a site or app, and 'login' (one word) for the noun, as in 'my login details'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraHey Ken, are you free to help me with something? I'm trying to book the meeting room.
KenSure. Is the booking page not loading?
AmiraIt loads, but it keeps sending me back to the start. I don't think I can get in.
KenDid you log in first? You have to use your work email, not your personal one.
AmiraOh! I was using my personal email. That's why.
KenEasy mistake. Try again with the work one.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you had trouble using a website or an app. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I tried to pay a bill online, but I couldn't log in because I'd forgotten my password. I had to reset it by email, which took about ten minutes. In the end I paid the bill, but I now use a password manager so it doesn't happen again.

Answer key: log in

  1. 1. D. Enter your details so you can use a website or system
  2. 2. log in
  3. 3. B. Have you tried to log in with your new password?
  4. 4. Example: Last month I tried to pay a bill online, but I couldn't log in because I'd forgotten my password. I had to reset it by email, which took about ten minutes. In the end I paid the bill, but I now use a password manager so it doesn't happen again.
