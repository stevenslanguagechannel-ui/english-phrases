on the receiving end of

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralEveryday life

The person who gets something, often something unpleasant.

Kofi was on the receiving end of a very angry email.

Tip: Use it with 'be' and a noun or '-ing' phrase: 'on the receiving end of a complaint / of someone's anger'. It usually describes something negative, so avoid it for good news unless you are joking.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraYou look tired. Was it a rough shift at the shop?
DiegoHonestly, yes. A customer was furious about a late delivery, and I was on the receiving end of it for twenty minutes.
AmiraOh no. Was it even your fault?
DiegoNot at all. The courier lost the parcel, but I was the only person standing there.
AmiraThat's so unfair. Let's get some food, and you can tell me everything.

Tell me about a time when you had to deal with someone else's bad mood or complaint. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I worked in a busy café, and one morning I was on the receiving end of a really angry complaint about a cold coffee. I stayed calm, apologised, and made a fresh one. In the end the customer smiled and even left a tip.

Answer key: on the receiving end of

  1. 1. C. The person who had to deal with someone's anger
  2. 2. on the receiving end of
  3. 3. C. Oh dear. It's not nice to be on the receiving end of a cold shoulder, even when you were polite.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I worked in a busy café, and one morning I was on the receiving end of a really angry complaint about a cold coffee. I stayed calm, apologised, and made a fresh one. In the end the customer smiled and even left a tip.
