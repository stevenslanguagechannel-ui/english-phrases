skip class

ExpressionLevel B1CasualWork & study

To not go to a class that you should go to.

I skipped class yesterday because I was tired, and now I have to catch up.

Tip: Use it as 'skip class' or 'skip a lesson' (not 'skip the school'); it usually means you chose not to go, so it suggests you weren't forced to stay away.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaWhere were you this morning? The seminar was really useful.
KofiI know, I feel bad. I decided to skip class and finish my essay instead.
OlenaDid you finish it?
KofiAlmost. But now I have no idea what I missed.
OlenaDon't worry, I took notes. You can copy them tonight.

Show an example answer

Yes, once last year I decided to skip class because I had a deadline at work. I felt guilty, but I borrowed a friend's notes and caught up the next day. I don't make a habit of it, though.

Answer key: skip class

  1. 1. C. Choose not to attend a class you are supposed to attend
  2. 2. skip class
  3. 3. A. Honestly, I might skip class tonight and go to bed early.
  4. 4. Example: Yes, once last year I decided to skip class because I had a deadline at work. I felt guilty, but I borrowed a friend's notes and caught up the next day. I don't make a habit of it, though.
