spread yourself thin

IdiomLevel B2NeutralWork & study

To try to do too many things at once, so you can't do any of them well.

She joined four clubs and now she's spreading herself too thin.

Tip: Use it as 'spread yourself (too) thin', changing the pronoun to match the subject: 'I'm spreading myself too thin', 'He tends to spread himself thin'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you coming to the study group on Thursday?
DiegoI wish I could, but I've got my evening classes, the extra shifts at the café, and my cousin's moving next week.
OlenaThat's a lot. Honestly, I think you're spreading yourself thin.
DiegoI know. I said yes to everything, and now my exam notes are a mess.
OlenaMaybe drop one thing for a few weeks? Even just the extra shifts.
DiegoYeah, you're right. I'll talk to my manager tomorrow.

Show an example answer

Yes, last year I was studying full time, working part time and helping at a community project. I was really spreading myself thin, and I started missing deadlines. In the end I stepped back from the project so I could focus on what mattered most.
