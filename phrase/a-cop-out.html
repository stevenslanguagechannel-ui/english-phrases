a cop-out

ExpressionLevel B2

An excuse or choice that avoids something difficult or that you should do.

Saying you forgot is a cop-out. You just didn't want to call.

A cop-out is a weak excuse to avoid something difficult. It's a noun: 'That's a cop-out.'
