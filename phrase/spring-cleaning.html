spring cleaning

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

A big, thorough cleaning of a house, typically done in the spring.

I'm doing my spring cleaning this weekend. I'll clear out the closet.

Tip: Often used as 'do your spring cleaning' or 'spring cleaning' as a noun phrase; people also use it loosely for any big tidy-up of a home, a desk or even a phone, in any season.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDo you want to come over on Saturday? I'm free all day.
KofiI'd love to, but I can't this weekend. I've put off my spring cleaning for weeks.
OlenaOh, I know that feeling. Is it just your flat, or the storage unit too?
KofiJust the flat, thankfully. But I'm going to empty every cupboard and wash all the curtains.
OlenaWow, that's a proper job. Text me when you're done and we'll go for lunch.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'spring cleaning' mean?

2. Fill the gap

My parents always start their in March, when the windows can finally stay open all day.

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: Sorry I haven't replied. My whole weekend disappeared into the flat.

4. Your turn: speak

Do you prefer to keep your home tidy all the time, or do one big clean now and then? Tell me about it.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I'm honestly more of a big-clean person. I let things pile up for a while, and then I do a proper spring cleaning, even if it's in the middle of autumn. It's tiring, but I love the feeling of a fresh, empty space afterwards.

Answer key: spring cleaning

  1. 1. B. A thorough clean of the whole home, with cupboards emptied and curtains washed
  2. 2. spring cleaning
  3. 3. C. Did you do your spring cleaning? Clearing out every cupboard takes ages!
  4. 4. Example: I'm honestly more of a big-clean person. I let things pile up for a while, and then I do a proper spring cleaning, even if it's in the middle of autumn. It's tiring, but I love the feeling of a fresh, empty space afterwards.
