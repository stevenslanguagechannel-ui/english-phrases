jump the queue

IdiomLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To jump the queue is to go in front of people who were waiting before you.

Please don't jump the queue. We have all been waiting for an hour.

Tip: Use it as a verb phrase: 'jump the queue' (British) or 'cut in line' (American); people also say 'queue-jumper' for the person doing it.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you get your parcel from the post office?
KofiNot yet. I waited for forty minutes, and then a man walked straight to the counter.
AmiraSeriously? Did he say anything?
KofiHe said he was in a hurry. He tried to jump the queue, but the cashier sent him to the back.
AmiraGood. Everyone was in a hurry.

Show an example answer

Last month I was waiting at a busy train station ticket machine, and a man just jumped the queue and started using it. I was annoyed because we had all been standing there for ten minutes. A few of us told him politely that there was a line, and he finally went to the back.

Answer key: jump the queue

  1. 1. B. Go in front of people who were waiting before you
  2. 2. jump the queue
  3. 3. A. Wow, she really jumped the queue. You should say something.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I was waiting at a busy train station ticket machine, and a man just jumped the queue and started using it. I was annoyed because we had all been standing there for ten minutes. A few of us told him politely that there was a line, and he finally went to the back.
