a hard pill to swallow

IdiomLevel B2

A fact or piece of news that is difficult or upsetting to accept.

Losing the final by one point was a hard pill to swallow.

A hard pill to swallow is bad news that is difficult to accept. Say 'It's a hard pill to swallow.'
