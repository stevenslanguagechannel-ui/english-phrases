time to kill

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralTime & effort

If you have time to kill, you have free time while you wait for something.

Our train is late, so we have an hour to kill. Let's get a snack.

Tip: Use it as 'have time to kill' or 'some time to kill' when you are waiting and have nothing urgent to do; it is not used for long periods or for time you chose to spend.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaIs your appointment at three? It's only half past one.
KofiI know. The bus was early, so now I've got ages.
OlenaThere's a little bookshop round the corner.
KofiGood idea. I've got time to kill, and I need a present for my sister.
OlenaThen take your time. Text me when you're done.
