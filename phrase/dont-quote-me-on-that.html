don't quote me on that

ExpressionLevel B1CasualOpinions & honesty

Used to say you are not completely sure about what you just said.

I think the train leaves at six, but don't quote me on that.

Tip: Add it after a fact you are not sure about, often with 'but': 'I think it's on Friday, but don't quote me on that.' Don't use it for things you know are true.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDo you know if the pharmacy on Mill Street is open on Sundays?
KofiI think it closes at two, but don't quote me on that.
AmiraTwo is fine. I only need to pick up some cough medicine.
KofiI walked past last month and saw the sign. Check their website first, though.
AmiraGood idea. I'll look before I leave the house.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you gave someone information but weren't completely sure it was right.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last week a colleague asked me when the office closes on public holidays. I said I thought it was at three, but don't quote me on that. I checked later and I was an hour off, so I was glad I had warned them.

Answer key: don't quote me on that

  1. 1. B. I'm not completely sure this is right, so please check it.
  2. 2. don't quote me on that
  3. 3. B. About three hours, I think, but don't quote me on that.
  4. 4. Example: Last week a colleague asked me when the office closes on public holidays. I said I thought it was at three, but don't quote me on that. I checked later and I was an hour off, so I was glad I had warned them.
