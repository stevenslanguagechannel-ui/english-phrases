slip my mind

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

If something slips your mind, you forget it without meaning to.

Sorry I missed your call. Your birthday totally slipped my mind!

Tip: Use 'something slips my/your/his mind' with the thing as the subject; don't say 'I slipped my mind', and it's usually used for small, ordinary things, not serious ones.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHey Kofi, did you manage to pick up the parcel from the post office?
KofiOh no. It completely slipped my mind. I walked right past it on my way home.
OlenaNo worries. Is it still open?
KofiUntil six, I think. I'll go back now.
OlenaGreat. Thanks, I need those papers by tomorrow.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you forgot to do something small but important. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I was supposed to renew my bus pass, but it totally slipped my mind. I only noticed when the driver asked to see it, which was pretty embarrassing. Now I set a reminder on my phone for things like that.

Answer key: slip my mind

  1. 1. A. I forgot to do it without meaning to.
  2. 2. slipped my mind
  3. 3. C. Sorry, it completely slipped my mind. I'll do it right now.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I was supposed to renew my bus pass, but it totally slipped my mind. I only noticed when the driver asked to see it, which was pretty embarrassing. Now I set a reminder on my phone for things like that.
