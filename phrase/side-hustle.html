side hustle

ExpressionLevel B2CasualWork & study

A small extra job you do in addition to your main job.

Amira sells handmade candles as a side hustle.

Tip: Use it as a countable noun: 'have a side hustle', 'start a side hustle', 'do something as a side hustle'. It often suggests a way to earn extra money, and people usually say it about their own or a friend's small business.

Hear it in conversation

KenYou look tired today. Did you stay up late again?
OlenaYes, I was editing photos until almost one.
KenFor work? I thought you finished at six.
OlenaNot for my office job. I've started a side hustle doing wedding photography on weekends.
KenThat's great! Is it going well?
OlenaSo far, yes. I already have four bookings for next spring.
