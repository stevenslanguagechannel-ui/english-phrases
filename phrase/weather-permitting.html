weather permitting

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralEveryday life

If the weather is good enough.

We'll have the party in the garden, weather permitting.

Tip: Put it at the end or start of a sentence as a short add-on after a plan; it says the plan depends on the weather, and it works in formal speech too.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre we still on for the hike on Saturday?
KofiI hope so. I've packed the car and booked the car park.
OlenaGreat. Should I bring the big camera?
KofiYes, bring it. We'll leave at eight, weather permitting.
OlenaOkay. If it rains, I'll text you first thing.
KofiPerfect. Let's decide by seven on Saturday morning.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a plan you have for the next few weeks that depends on something outside your control.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I'm hoping to repaint our garden fence next weekend, weather permitting. It needs a few dry days in a row, so I'm checking the forecast every morning. If it rains, I'll just move it to the following weekend.

Answer key: weather permitting

  1. 1. D. If the weather is good enough
  2. 2. weather permitting
  3. 3. C. Yes, weather permitting. The forecast looks a bit grey, though.
  4. 4. Example: I'm hoping to repaint our garden fence next weekend, weather permitting. It needs a few dry days in a row, so I'm checking the forecast every morning. If it rains, I'll just move it to the following weekend.
