burning a hole in your pocket

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

You have money and want to spend it right away.

I got my bonus today and it's burning a hole in my pocket.

Tip: Use it for money you have just received or are about to spend; it is usually 'it's burning a hole in my pocket', and the person may be impatient to shop.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraAre you coming into town on Saturday? I'm meeting Olena for lunch.
DiegoMaybe. I need to go to the shops anyway.
AmiraOh, what are you looking for?
DiegoNothing in particular. My tax refund came through this morning and it's burning a hole in my pocket.
AmiraHa, don't spend it all in one afternoon! Maybe put some aside first.
DiegoYou're right. I'll try. But I really want those new headphones.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "burning a hole in my pocket" mean?

2. Fill the gap

She got paid on Friday, and by Saturday morning the money was already and she was online looking at new shoes.

3. Choose the best reply

Kofi: Did you buy anything while you were on holiday?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you received some money and wanted to spend it straight away. What did you do?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I got a small bonus at work, and honestly it was burning a hole in my pocket for the whole weekend. I tried to be sensible, but in the end I spent half of it on a new jacket I'd wanted for ages. I put the rest into my savings, so I didn't feel too guilty.

Answer key: burning a hole in your pocket

  1. 1. D. Diego has money and wants to spend it right away.
  2. 2. burning a hole in her pocket
  3. 3. B. Not much. I'm trying to save, but my birthday money was burning a hole in my pocket, so I got a few souvenirs.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I got a small bonus at work, and honestly it was burning a hole in my pocket for the whole weekend. I tried to be sensible, but in the end I spent half of it on a new jacket I'd wanted for ages. I put the rest into my savings, so I didn't feel too guilty.
