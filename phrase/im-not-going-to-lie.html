I'm not going to lie

ExpressionLevel B1CasualOpinions & honesty

A spoken phrase that means 'to be honest' before you say something true.

I'm not going to lie, that exam was really hard.

Tip: Put it before the true (often negative or surprising) thing you want to say, then a comma: "I'm not going to lie, …"; it's very common in speech, but in formal writing use "to be honest" or "frankly".

Hear it in conversation

AmiraYou tried the new canteen, right? What did you think of the pasta?
DiegoI'm not going to lie, I've had better. It was a bit cold in the middle.
AmiraOh no. I was going to go there for lunch today.
DiegoMaybe try the soup instead. Other people said it was really good.
AmiraOkay, thanks. I'll give it a go.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you tried something new and it was harder than you expected.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I started learning to swim as an adult, and I'm not going to lie, I felt really embarrassed at first. Everyone else in the class seemed so confident. But after a few weeks I relaxed, and now I actually look forward to it.

Answer key: I'm not going to lie

  1. 1. B. I'm being honest with you, even though my opinion isn't very positive.
  2. 2. I'm not going to lie
  3. 3. A. I'm not going to lie, I'm a bit stressed about all the packing.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I started learning to swim as an adult, and I'm not going to lie, I felt really embarrassed at first. Everyone else in the class seemed so confident. But after a few weeks I relaxed, and now I actually look forward to it.
