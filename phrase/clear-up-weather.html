clear up (weather)

Phrasal verbLevel B1CasualEveryday life

When the weather clears up, clouds, rain or fog go away and it gets brighter.

The sky should clear up by lunchtime, so let's wait before we go hiking.

Tip: Use it with 'it' or a weather word as the subject (the sky, the fog, the rain); it describes weather getting better, and you can add 'by' + a time to say when.

Hear it in conversation

KofiIs the ferry still running? I can barely see the water this morning.
AmiraYes, but the fog is thick. They said it might not leave until ten.
KofiThat's a shame. I wanted to get to the island before the crowds.
AmiraLet's grab a coffee first. It usually starts to clear up around nine here.
KofiGood idea. If it does, we can still catch the second boat.

Answer key: clear up (weather)

  1. 1. C. The fog goes away and the weather gets brighter
  2. 2. cleared up
  3. 3. C. Don't worry, the forecast says it'll clear up by noon.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer we planned a beach day, but it rained heavily in the morning. We waited at a café for two hours, and luckily it cleared up around lunchtime, so we still got a few hours of sun.
