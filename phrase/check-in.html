check in

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To tell a hotel or airline that you have arrived and get your room or boarding pass.

We need to check in at the hotel before noon.

Tip: Use 'check in' without an object (check in at the desk, check in online); the noun is 'check-in' and the opposite is 'check out'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraOur flight to Lisbon leaves at nine, so we should leave the house by six.
KofiFine. Can we check in online tonight instead of at the airport?
AmiraGood idea. Then we can just drop our bags and go straight to security.
KofiPerfect. I'll find our booking number and do it now.
AmiraThanks. Don't forget to download the boarding passes to your phone.

Show an example answer

Last summer I travelled to the coast with my cousin. We arrived early, so we couldn't check in at the hotel until the afternoon. The staff kept our bags safe, and we spent the morning walking along the beach.

  2. 2. check in
