make do

Phrasal verbLevel B2NeutralProblems & solutions

To manage with what you have, even if it isn't perfect.

We forgot the milk, so we'll have to make do with water.

Tip: Use 'make do with + thing' for what you have instead, or 'make do without + thing' when something is missing; it's common in everyday speech and fine in most situations.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid the hardware shop have the shelf brackets you wanted?
KofiNo, they were sold out. And the shop closes in ten minutes.
OlenaSo what are you going to do about the bookshelf?
KofiI'll just make do with the old screws for now and buy the proper ones on Saturday.
OlenaFine, as long as the books don't end up on the floor!
