touch down

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

When a plane touches down, its wheels land on the ground.

Our plane touches down at seven, so I'll text you then.

Tip: Use 'touch down' for planes, helicopters and spacecraft arriving on the ground; the subject is the aircraft, and it often comes with a time or a place ('touch down in Lisbon at noon').

Hear it in conversation

AmiraIs your flight still on time? I'm leaving for the airport soon.
KofiThe app says we should touch down around 6:40, but there's a bit of wind.
AmiraNo problem. I'll wait by the arrivals doors.
KofiGreat. It'll take me a while to get my suitcase, so don't panic if I'm late.
AmiraI won't. Just message me when you're out.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you were waiting for someone to arrive from a long journey.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

My cousin once flew in from Canada to visit us, and I went to the airport to meet her. Her plane was supposed to touch down at noon, but it was late, so I waited for almost two hours with a coffee. When she finally came out, we were both so happy that I forgot about the wait.

Answer key: touch down

  1. 1. B. Land on the runway after a flight
  2. 2. touch down
  3. 3. C. Yes, her plane should touch down at about nine now.
  4. 4. Example: My cousin once flew in from Canada to visit us, and I went to the airport to meet her. Her plane was supposed to touch down at noon, but it was late, so I waited for almost two hours with a coffee. When she finally came out, we were both so happy that I forgot about the wait.
