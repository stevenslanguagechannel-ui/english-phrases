get a workout in

ExpressionLevel B1CasualTime & effort

To manage to fit exercise (or another activity) into a busy day.

I was busy all day, but I still got a quick walk in at lunch.

Tip: Use it as 'get a workout in' (or 'get a run/walk/swim in') when you manage to exercise despite a full schedule; it is often used with 'before work', 'at lunch' or 'today'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaYou look energetic today. Didn't you have meetings from nine to five?
KofiI did, but I got up early and got a workout in before the first call.
OlenaSeriously? I can barely answer emails at that time.
KofiIt was only twenty minutes at home. It makes the whole day feel easier.
OlenaMaybe I should try that. Evenings never work for me.

How do you stay active when you have a very busy schedule?

Show an example answer

It isn't easy, but I try to get a workout in whenever I have a free half hour. Usually I go for a fast walk at lunch or do a short home routine before work. Even ten minutes helps me feel less stressed.

Answer key: get a workout in

  1. 1. B. Managed to fit exercise into a busy day
  2. 2. got a quick swim in
  3. 3. A. Not really, but I got a short workout in at home on Tuesday.
