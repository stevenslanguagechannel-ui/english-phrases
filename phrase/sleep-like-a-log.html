sleep like a log

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To sleep very well and deeply.

I was so tired that I slept like a log.

Tip: Use it in the past to describe a night you slept well, as in 'I slept like a log', or in the present for a habit, as in 'My brother sleeps like a log'. Don't use it for a short nap or for broken sleep.

Hear it in conversation

KenYou look so fresh this morning, Olena. How was the night train?
OlenaHonestly, brilliant. I slept like a log until the guard woke us up.
KenReally? I could hear the wheels all night, and I barely slept.
OlenaI had my earplugs in. Plus I'd walked around the city all day.
KenNext time I'm borrowing your earplugs.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you were very tired. How did you sleep afterwards?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I helped my cousin move house, and we carried boxes up and down stairs all day. By the evening I was completely exhausted, so I went to bed early and slept like a log. I didn't wake up until almost ten the next morning.

Answer key: sleep like a log

  1. 1. B. Slept very deeply and well
  2. 2. slept like a log
  3. 3. C. Not at all, I slept like a log.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I helped my cousin move house, and we carried boxes up and down stairs all day. By the evening I was completely exhausted, so I went to bed early and slept like a log. I didn't wake up until almost ten the next morning.
