read between the lines

IdiomLevel B2NeutralOpinions & honesty

To understand the hidden meaning that someone doesn't say directly.

The email was polite, but if you read between the lines, he's not happy.

Tip: Use it as a verb phrase, often with 'if you' or 'when you' ('if you read between the lines'); the hidden meaning is usually negative or something the speaker is avoiding saying.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you hear back from the landlord about the broken heater?
DiegoYes, he said he'd 'look into it when he has a chance.'
AmiraThat sounds hopeful.
DiegoNot really. When you read between the lines, he's not planning to fix it soon.
AmiraAh. Maybe we should send a formal message so there's a record.
DiegoGood idea. I'll write it tonight.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'read between the lines' mean?

2. Fill the gap

My manager said the team 'might look at other options next year', and if you , I think my project is being cancelled.

3. Choose the best reply

Kofi: My neighbour said she'd 'love to come to the party if she's free', but she didn't ask what time it starts.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when someone didn't say what they really thought, and you had to guess it.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

A few months ago a colleague said my presentation was 'interesting', but she didn't say anything else. I read between the lines and realised she thought it was too long, so I cut ten minutes before the next meeting. She seemed much happier with the shorter version.

Answer key: read between the lines

  1. 1. B. To understand what someone really means even though they don't say it directly
  2. 2. read between the lines
  3. 3. A. I think she was being polite. If you read between the lines, she probably won't come.
  4. 4. Example: A few months ago a colleague said my presentation was 'interesting', but she didn't say anything else. I read between the lines and realised she thought it was too long, so I cut ten minutes before the next meeting. She seemed much happier with the shorter version.
