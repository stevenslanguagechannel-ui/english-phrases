keep in mind

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralProblems & solutions

To remember an important fact when you make a decision or plan.

Keep in mind that the shop closes at six.

Tip: Use it with a 'that' clause or 'the fact that': 'Keep in mind (that) the trains are slower on Sundays.' It's also common as a reminder to yourself: 'I'll keep that in mind.'

Hear it in conversation

AmiraI'm thinking of booking the early flight to Lisbon. It's much cheaper.
KofiSure, but keep in mind that the first metro doesn't start until six.
AmiraOh, I hadn't thought of that. The flight leaves at 6:30.
KofiExactly. You'd probably need a taxi to the airport, and that costs a lot.
AmiraHmm. Then the savings might disappear. Maybe I'll take the later flight.

Answer key: keep in mind

  1. 1. B. Remember an important fact when you decide something
  2. 2. keep in mind
  3. 3. A. Great idea. Keep in mind that it's supposed to rain all week.
