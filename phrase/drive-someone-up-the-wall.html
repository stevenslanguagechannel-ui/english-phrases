drive someone up the wall

IdiomLevel B2CasualFeelings & reactions

To annoy someone very much.

The noise from my neighbor's speaker drives me up the wall.

Tip: Use it as 'X drives me up the wall' or 'It drives me up the wall when…'; it's informal, so save it for friends and colleagues rather than formal writing.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaYou look tired, Kofi. Did you sleep badly?
KofiYes. The people upstairs were moving furniture until midnight again.
OlenaAgain? Have you spoken to them?
KofiI left a note, but nothing changed. Honestly, it's starting to drive me up the wall.
OlenaI'd go and knock on their door. Maybe they don't realise how much sound travels.
KofiYou're right. I'll try that tonight.

Is there a small habit or noise that annoys you? Tell me about it.

Show an example answer

Yes, I really can't stand loud chewing. My colleague eats crisps at his desk all afternoon, and it drives me up the wall. I try to ignore it, but sometimes I put my headphones on.

Answer key: drive someone up the wall

  1. 1. B. It makes me extremely annoyed
  2. 2. driving me up the wall
  3. 3. C. Ken kept interrupting everyone, and it was driving me up the wall.
  4. 4. Example: Yes, I really can't stand loud chewing. My colleague eats crisps at his desk all afternoon, and it drives me up the wall. I try to ignore it, but sometimes I put my headphones on.
