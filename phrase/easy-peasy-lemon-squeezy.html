easy peasy, lemon squeezy

IdiomLevel B1

Very, very easy. It is a playful, rhyming phrase.

Setting up the new printer was easy peasy, lemon squeezy.

'Easy peasy, lemon squeezy' means something is very easy. Use it in friendly, informal talk.
