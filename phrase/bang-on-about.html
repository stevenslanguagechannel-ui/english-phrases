bang on about

Phrasal verbLevel B2

To talk about the same subject for too long, in a boring or annoying way.

Dad keeps banging on about the old days whenever we visit.

Bang on about means to talk about a topic for too long. It's informal and mostly British: 'He bangs on about football.'
