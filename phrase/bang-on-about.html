bang on about

Phrasal verbLevel B2CasualPeople & relationships

To talk about the same subject for too long, in a boring or annoying way.

Dad keeps banging on about the old days whenever we visit.

Tip: Use it with 'about' plus the subject (bang on about politics, bang on about work); it's mildly critical, so say it about other people more than to their face. It's also common in the form 'go banging on about'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraHow was the train ride with Kofi? Six hours is a long time.
KenHonestly, I lost the will to live around hour two.
AmiraWhy, what happened?
KenHe wouldn't stop banging on about his new running app. Steps, routes, heart rate, everything.
AmiraHa, I'd have put my headphones on straight away.
KenI did, eventually. He didn't even notice.

Answer key: bang on about

  1. 1. C. Talking about something for so long that it becomes boring
  2. 2. banging on about
  3. 3. C. It was fine, but one guy banged on about his holiday photos all evening.
  4. 4. Example: My uncle is a good example. Whenever we meet, he bangs on about his garden for at least half an hour, and I just nod and smile. He's a lovely person, but I've learned to change the subject quickly.
