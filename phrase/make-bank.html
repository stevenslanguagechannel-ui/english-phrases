make bank

IdiomLevel B2CasualSuccess & progress

To earn a lot of money quickly, usually in an informal way.

The food truck is making bank at the summer festival.

Tip: Use it as an informal verb phrase (make bank, is making bank, made bank) for people or businesses; it's slang, so avoid it in formal writing or serious reports.

Hear it in conversation

KofiDid you hear Amira sold her old camera gear online?
MeiYes! She told me she made bank. Someone paid triple what she expected.
KofiWow. I have a drawer full of old phones and cables.
MeiCheck what they're worth before you throw them out. Some of that stuff sells fast.
KofiOkay, I'll look this weekend. Maybe I'll finally afford a new laptop.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

2. Fill the gap

3. Choose the best reply

Olena: Did your brother's side business ever take off?

4. Your turn: speak

Do you know anyone who has earned a lot of money from a side project or a quick job? What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

My friend Diego started selling homemade hot sauce online during the holidays. He didn't expect much, but he made bank, and he sold out in two weeks. Now he's thinking about turning it into a real business.

Answer key: make bank

  1. 1. B. Earned a lot of money easily and quickly
  2. 2. making bank
  3. 3. B. Yes, he's making bank selling custom phone cases.
  4. 4. Example: My friend Diego started selling homemade hot sauce online during the holidays. He didn't expect much, but he made bank, and he sold out in two weeks. Now he's thinking about turning it into a real business.
