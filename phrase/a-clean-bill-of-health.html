a clean bill of health

IdiomLevel B2NeutralEveryday life

A doctor's report saying you are healthy.

The doctor gave me a clean bill of health.

Tip: Usually used with 'give someone' or 'get': 'The specialist gave her a clean bill of health' or 'He got a clean bill of health after the scan'; it also works for machines, buildings and companies, but it's most natural for people's health.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraYou look much better, Ken. How did the check-up go yesterday?
KenReally well, actually. After all those tests, the cardiologist said I'm in good shape.
AmiraSo you're completely in the clear?
KenYes, I walked out with a clean bill of health and a note to keep exercising.
AmiraThat's a huge relief. Let's celebrate with a long walk this weekend.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you were worried about your health or a family member's health. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

A few years ago my dad had chest pains, and the whole family was really anxious. He went through several tests at the hospital, and in the end the doctors gave him a clean bill of health. We were so relieved that we all went out for dinner to celebrate.

Answer key: a clean bill of health

  1. 1. D. An official statement from a doctor that you are healthy and have no problems
  2. 2. clean bill of health
  3. 3. A. That's great news! So you've got a clean bill of health.
  4. 4. Example: A few years ago my dad had chest pains, and the whole family was really anxious. He went through several tests at the hospital, and in the end the doctors gave him a clean bill of health. We were so relieved that we all went out for dinner to celebrate.
