splurge on

Phrasal verbLevel B2CasualEveryday life

To spend more money than usual on something you want.

I splurged on a really good coffee machine.

Tip: Use 'splurge on' + the thing you buy (splurge on new shoes); it suggests a treat you don't strictly need, and 'splurge' alone also works (let's splurge).

Hear it in conversation

AmiraYour flat looks amazing! Is that a new sofa?
KofiYes! I got a bonus at work, so I splurged on it.
AmiraGood for you. Was it very expensive?
KofiWay more than I planned, but it's so comfy. I don't regret it.
AmiraFair enough. You work hard, you deserve it.

4. Your turn: speak

Is there something you like to spend extra money on, even when you're trying to save? Tell me about it.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I'm usually careful with money, but I always splurge on good running shoes. I run a few times a week, and comfortable shoes really make a difference, so I think they're worth it.

Answer key: splurge on

  1. 1. A. Spent more money than usual on something he wanted
  2. 2. splurge on
  3. 3. C. They were, but it's winter and I decided to splurge on something good.
  4. 4. Example: I'm usually careful with money, but I always splurge on good running shoes. I run a few times a week, and comfortable shoes really make a difference, so I think they're worth it.
