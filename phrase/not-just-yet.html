not just yet

ExpressionLevel B1CasualTime & effort

Not now, but maybe soon.

Can I order now? Not just yet, I'm still deciding.

Tip: Use it as a short reply when someone asks if you're ready or if something can start; it often comes before a reason, and 'just yet' makes the delay feel brief.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHey Kofi, are you ready to leave? The taxi's outside.
KofiNot just yet. I can't find my charger.
OlenaIs it in your bag?
KofiI checked there. Oh, wait, it's under the sofa. Got it!
OlenaGreat. Two minutes, then we go?
KofiTwo minutes. I just need to grab my coat.
