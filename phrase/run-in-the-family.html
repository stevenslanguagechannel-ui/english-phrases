run in the family

IdiomLevel B1NeutralPeople & relationships

A feature or habit is common in a family because it passes from parent to child.

Curly hair runs in my family.

Tip: Use 'run in the family' (or 'run in families') for traits, talents, habits or health conditions passed down; the subject is the thing that is inherited, e.g. 'Musical talent runs in the family', not the person.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraKen, I saw your sister's drawings at the party. They were amazing!
KenThanks! She's been painting since she was five.
AmiraDo you draw too?
KenA bit. My mum and my grandfather were both artists, so I think it runs in the family.
AmiraThat makes sense. I can barely draw a straight line!

4. Your turn: speak

Is there a talent, habit or health issue that is common in your family? Tell me about it.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Yes, a good sense of humour definitely runs in my family. My dad is always telling jokes, and now my brother and I do the same. Even my grandmother makes everyone laugh at dinner.

Answer key: run in the family

  1. 1. A. Many people in the family have this talent, and it is passed down
  2. 2. runs in the family
  3. 3. C. Yes, we're all very tall. It runs in the family.
  4. 4. Example: Yes, a good sense of humour definitely runs in my family. My dad is always telling jokes, and now my brother and I do the same. Even my grandmother makes everyone laugh at dinner.
