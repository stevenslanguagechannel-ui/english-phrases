next to impossible

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralProblems & solutions

Almost impossible, extremely difficult.

Finding a quiet seat on the bus at 8 a.m. is next to impossible.

Tip: Use it before an adjective or infinitive phrase: 'next to impossible to find' or 'next to impossible for us'; it sounds a little softer than 'impossible' because it leaves a tiny gap.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid you manage to book a table for Saturday? It's Amira's birthday.
DiegoI tried five places. Everything is full.
OlenaEven the Italian place near the station?
DiegoYes, it's next to impossible to get a table for ten on a weekend.
OlenaThen let's skip the restaurant. I can cook at my place.
DiegoThat's a great idea. I'll bring dessert.

Show an example answer

In my city, finding an affordable flat near the centre is next to impossible. Prices keep rising, and there are always dozens of people applying for the same place. Most of my friends end up living further out and commuting.

Answer key: next to impossible

  1. 1. C. Almost impossible; extremely difficult
  2. 2. next to impossible
  3. 3. A. I know, getting through to them is next to impossible.
  4. 4. Example: In my city, finding an affordable flat near the centre is next to impossible. Prices keep rising, and there are always dozens of people applying for the same place. Most of my friends end up living further out and commuting.
