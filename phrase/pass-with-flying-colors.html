pass with flying colors

IdiomLevel B2NeutralSuccess & progress

To pass a test very easily and with a very high score.

She passed her driving test with flying colors.

Tip: Use it with 'pass' (or 'come through', 'get through') plus an exam, inspection or assessment: 'She passed the inspection with flying colors.' Don't use it for things you can't pass, like a holiday or a meal. The British spelling 'colours' is also correct.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaKofi, you look relaxed. Did the food safety inspection go okay yesterday?
KofiBetter than okay. The inspector checked the whole kitchen and we passed with flying colors.
OlenaReally? Last year they found a few problems with the fridges.
KofiI know! This time we fixed everything beforehand, so there was nothing to write down.
OlenaThat's great news. The whole team deserves a treat.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'passed with flying colors' mean?

2. Fill the gap

My brother was nervous about his first-aid course, but he came through it .

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: Did your nephew pass his piano exam last week?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you were nervous about something you had to do, but it went really well.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last spring I had to give a presentation to my whole department, and I was really nervous. I practiced for a week, and in the end I passed the assessment with flying colors. My manager even asked me to train the new staff afterwards.

Answer key: pass with flying colors

  1. 1. A. Passed the inspection easily, with no problems at all
  2. 2. with flying colors
  3. 3. C. Yes, he passed with flying colors. The examiner gave him almost full marks.
  4. 4. Example: Last spring I had to give a presentation to my whole department, and I was really nervous. I practiced for a week, and in the end I passed the assessment with flying colors. My manager even asked me to train the new staff afterwards.
