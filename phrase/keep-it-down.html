keep it down

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To make less noise.

Could you keep it down? The baby is sleeping.

Tip: Use it as a polite request ('Could you keep it down?') or a mild order ('Keep it down, please'); it works for voices and sounds like music or TV, not for volume settings you can see.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraSorry I'm late. Why are you whispering?
KofiMy flatmate has an online interview in the next room.
AmiraOh no. Should I go outside and wait?
KofiNo, come in. Just keep it down until she's finished, okay?
AmiraOf course. I'll even take my shoes off.
KofiThanks. I'll make us some tea in the meantime.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when noise bothered you at home or while studying. What did you do?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year my neighbours had friends over on a weeknight, and I had an exam the next morning. I knocked on their door and politely asked them to keep it down. They apologised straight away and the noise stopped, so I was able to get some sleep.

Answer key: keep it down

  1. 1. C. Speak and move quietly so you don't disturb someone
  2. 2. keep it down
  3. 3. B. Oh, sorry! I'll keep it down.
  4. 4. Example: Last year my neighbours had friends over on a weeknight, and I had an exam the next morning. I knocked on their door and politely asked them to keep it down. They apologised straight away and the noise stopped, so I was able to get some sleep.
