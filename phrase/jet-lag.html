jet lag

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

Jet lag is the tired, confused feeling you get after a long flight to a different time zone.

I always get terrible jet lag when I fly to Tokyo.

Tip: Use it as an uncountable noun: 'I have jet lag' or 'suffering from jet lag', not 'a jet lag'; you can also say 'get over jet lag' or 'beat jet lag'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaYou look exhausted, Kofi. Weren't you back from Lagos on Sunday?
KofiI was, but I'm still waking up at three in the morning.
OlenaIs it the time difference?
KofiYes, I think it's jet lag. I'm sleepy all afternoon and wide awake at night.
OlenaTry getting outside in the morning light. That helped me last year.
KofiGood idea. I'll go for a walk before work tomorrow.

Show an example answer

Last spring I flew to Mexico City, and I had really bad jet lag for almost a week. I was falling asleep at dinner and waking up at four in the morning. What helped was spending time outside in the daylight and going to bed at the local time, even when I wasn't sleepy.
