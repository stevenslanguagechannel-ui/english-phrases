on my behalf

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralWork & study

Instead of someone, or for them, as their representative.

My manager asked me to accept the award on her behalf.

Tip: Use 'on someone's behalf' (or 'on behalf of someone') before or after the verb: 'sign on my behalf', 'speak on behalf of the team'; don't confuse it with 'on behalf' used alone or with 'in favour of'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you still coming to the landlord meeting on Thursday?
KofiI can't, I'll be at the airport. Could you talk to him on my behalf?
OlenaSure. What do you want me to say?
KofiJust ask when they'll fix the heating. And please write down what he says.
OlenaNo problem. I'll message you right after the meeting.

4. Your turn: speak

Have you ever asked someone to do something for you because you couldn't do it yourself? What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I was travelling when a parcel arrived, so I asked my neighbour to collect it on my behalf. She had to show a message with my name and number, but it worked well. When I got home, the parcel was waiting for me on the kitchen table.

Answer key: on my behalf

  1. 1. B. Instead of me, as my representative
  2. 2. on his behalf
  3. 3. B. Of course. Do you want me to introduce the project on your behalf?
  4. 4. Example: Last year I was travelling when a parcel arrived, so I asked my neighbour to collect it on my behalf. She had to show a message with my name and number, but it worked well. When I got home, the parcel was waiting for me on the kitchen table.
