come across

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To find or meet something by chance.

I came across an old photo while cleaning my room.

Tip: Use 'come across' + a thing or person you find unexpectedly (I came across it); don't use it for something you were looking for on purpose, and don't confuse it with 'come across as' (to seem).

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you ever find that recipe your grandmother used to make?
KofiYes! I came across it yesterday in a box of old books.
AmiraReally? I thought you'd lost it years ago.
KofiSo did I. I was only looking for a charger.
AmiraThat's lucky. Are you going to cook it this weekend?
