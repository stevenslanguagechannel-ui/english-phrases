burn off

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To use up calories or extra energy by doing exercise.

I walk to work to burn off some energy.

Tip: Use it with the thing you lose: burn off calories, fat or energy. You can put the object in the middle (burn it off) or after (burn off the pasta).

Hear it in conversation

AmiraAre you seriously going for a run? It's freezing out there.
DiegoI know, but I ate way too much at my cousin's wedding yesterday.
AmiraHa! So this is a punishment?
DiegoNot really. I just want to burn off all that cake before I sit at my desk all day.
AmiraFair enough. Take a hat, though.

4. Your turn: speak

How do you relax or release stress after a long day at work or study?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I usually go for a long walk or a quick swim to burn off the stress. If I skip exercise, I find it hard to switch off in the evening. Even twenty minutes helps me burn off that restless energy.

Answer key: burn off

  1. 1. A. To use up extra calories by being active
  2. 2. burn off
  3. 3. C. Let's take a quick walk to burn it off.
  4. 4. Example: I usually go for a long walk or a quick swim to burn off the stress. If I skip exercise, I find it hard to switch off in the evening. Even twenty minutes helps me burn off that restless energy.
