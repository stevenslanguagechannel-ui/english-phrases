hold the line

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

Hold the line is what you say to ask someone to wait on the phone.

Please hold the line. I'll check that for you.

Tip: Use it as a polite request on the phone: 'Could you hold the line, please?' or 'Please hold the line.' It's most common in customer service and offices, and 'hold on' is the more casual version.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHello, City Dental. How can I help?
KofiHi, I'd like to move my appointment from Thursday to Friday.
OlenaOf course. Could you hold the line while I check the calendar?
KofiSure, no problem.
OlenaThanks for waiting. We have a space on Friday at ten.
KofiPerfect, I'll take it.

Tell me about a time you had to wait on the phone to get help with something. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month my internet stopped working, so I called my provider. The assistant asked me to hold the line while they checked my account, and I waited almost ten minutes. In the end they fixed the problem remotely, so it was worth the wait.

Answer key: hold the line

  1. 1. A. Stay on the phone and wait for a moment
  2. 2. hold the line
  3. 3. C. Of course. Please hold the line while I put you through.
  4. 4. Example: Last month my internet stopped working, so I called my provider. The assistant asked me to hold the line while they checked my account, and I waited almost ten minutes. In the end they fixed the problem remotely, so it was worth the wait.
