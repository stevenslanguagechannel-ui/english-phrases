get off (work / a shift)

Phrasal verbLevel B1CasualWork & study

To finish work for the day.

I get off work at six, so let's meet at seven.

Tip: Use it as get off + work/a shift (or just 'get off' with a time); it's about when you leave, so it's not the same as 'get off a bus'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraAre you free for dinner on Thursday?
KofiMaybe. I'm on the late shift that day.
AmiraOh no. What time do you get off?
KofiUsually around nine, but sometimes it's later.
AmiraThen let's do Friday instead. You'll be less tired.
KofiGood idea. Friday works.
