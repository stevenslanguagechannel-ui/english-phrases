shop till you drop

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To shop for a very long time until you are completely tired.

We went to the sales and shopped till we dropped.

Tip: Use it as a verb phrase with a past or present form ("shopped till they dropped"), usually about a long, tiring shopping trip; it's playful, so it's not for serious or formal writing.

Hear it in conversation

KofiYou look exhausted. Did you get all your holiday presents yesterday?
AmiraAlmost all of them. My sister and I went into town at nine and shopped till we dropped.
KofiNine in the morning? When did you finish?
AmiraAround six. My feet still hurt, but I only have two gifts left to buy.
KofiWow. I'm ordering mine online this year.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you spent a whole day shopping. How did you feel afterwards?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last winter I went to a big sale with my friend, and we really shopped till we dropped. We were in the shops from ten in the morning until the evening, and by the end I could hardly walk. It was fun, but next time I'll plan a shorter trip.

Answer key: shop till you drop

  1. 1. C. They shopped for so long that they were completely worn out.
  2. 2. shop till they drop
  3. 3. B. Well, we shopped till we dropped, but I found the perfect jacket, so it was worth it.
  4. 4. Example: Last winter I went to a big sale with my friend, and we really shopped till we dropped. We were in the shops from ten in the morning until the evening, and by the end I could hardly walk. It was fun, but next time I'll plan a shorter trip.
