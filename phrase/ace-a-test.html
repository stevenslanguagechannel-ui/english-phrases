ace a test

ExpressionLevel B1CasualWork & study

To do extremely well on a test or exam.

I studied hard, and I aced the test!

Tip: Use it as a verb: 'ace a test/exam/interview/presentation'; the past form is 'aced', and it only describes a very good result, not just passing.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaYou look relieved, Kofi. How did the driving theory exam go?
KofiHonestly, better than I expected. I only got one question wrong.
OlenaWow, so you basically aced it!
KofiPretty much. I did practice questions on my phone every evening for a month.
OlenaThat explains it. Now you just need the practical part.
KofiDon't remind me. That's next Tuesday.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you felt really well prepared for something important, such as an exam, a presentation or an interview. How did it go?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I had to give a presentation at work in front of the whole department. I practised it with a friend about ten times, so I felt calm on the day, and I think I aced it. My manager even asked me to present it again at the next meeting.

Answer key: ace a test

  1. 1. C. Did very well on it, with an excellent result
  2. 2. aced
  3. 3. A. Wow, you really aced it! All that revision paid off.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I had to give a presentation at work in front of the whole department. I practised it with a friend about ten times, so I felt calm on the day, and I think I aced it. My manager even asked me to present it again at the next meeting.
