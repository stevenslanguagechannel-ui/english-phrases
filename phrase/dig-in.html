dig in

Phrasal verbLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To start eating, usually with enthusiasm.

Dinner's ready, everyone. Dig in!

Tip: Use it as a phrasal verb with no object ("Dig in!" or "We dug in"); it's what people say when food arrives, and it sounds warm and informal, so avoid it in very formal settings.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaSorry I'm late, Kofi. The bus took forever.
KofiNo problem! The soup's still hot. Here's a bowl.
OlenaOh, that smells amazing. I'm starving.
KofiThen dig in. There's plenty of bread too.
OlenaThanks. I skipped lunch, so I won't be shy!

Answer key: dig in

  1. 1. A. Start eating, and enjoy it
  2. 2. dug in
  3. 3. C. Great, let's dig in before it gets cold.
