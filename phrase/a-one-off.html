a one-off

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralProblems & solutions

Something that happens only once and is not expected to happen again.

The late delivery was a one-off. It doesn't usually happen.

Tip: Use it with 'a' or 'was a': 'The mistake was a one-off.' It suggests a problem is not a pattern, so people often follow it with 'I hope' or 'it won't happen again'.

Hear it in conversation

KofiDid you hear? The Wi-Fi in the office went down again this morning.
AmiraAgain? I thought IT fixed it last month.
KofiThey did. They told me today's problem was a one-off, caused by a power cut in the building.
AmiraGood. I have a big video call at two, and I can't have it freezing.
KofiIt's working fine now. If you're worried, you could use the meeting room next to mine.
AmiraMaybe I will, just to be safe. Thanks!

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when something went wrong, but it didn't happen again.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last summer my flight was cancelled on the way to a family visit, which almost never happens to me. It was a one-off, because a storm closed the airport for a day. I've travelled that route many times since and never had a problem.

Answer key: a one-off

  1. 1. A. Something that happened one time and probably won't happen again
  2. 2. a one-off
  3. 3. C. Not really. Yesterday was a one-off, because my alarm didn't ring.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer my flight was cancelled on the way to a family visit, which almost never happens to me. It was a one-off, because a storm closed the airport for a day. I've travelled that route many times since and never had a problem.
