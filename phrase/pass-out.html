pass out

Phrasal verbLevel B1

To fall asleep suddenly because you are very tired, or to faint.

I was so tired after the trip that I passed out on the sofa.

Pass out means to faint, or informally to fall asleep suddenly because you are exhausted. Use the context to tell which one is meant.
