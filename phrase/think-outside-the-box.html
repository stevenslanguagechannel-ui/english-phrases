think outside the box

IdiomLevel B1NeutralProblems & solutions

To think in a new, creative way instead of the usual way.

We need to think outside the box to fix this problem.

Tip: Use it as a verb phrase: 'think outside the box' (to do it) or 'need to think outside the box'; the box is never a real object, so don't add a place or a size.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaOur flat is tiny, and I still need a desk for working from home.
KofiHave you measured the space under your bed?
OlenaYes, it's too low. I've tried every layout I can imagine.
KofiThen maybe you need to think outside the box. What about a desk that folds into the wall?
OlenaOh, I hadn't thought of that. It could work!

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you had to solve a problem in an unusual way.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year my team had no budget for a proper event, so we had to think outside the box. Instead of booking a venue, we held it online and asked our customers to share short videos. It cost almost nothing, and more people took part than we expected.

Answer key: think outside the box

  1. 1. A. Look for a new, creative idea instead of the usual solutions
  2. 2. think outside the box
  3. 3. C. Maybe we should think outside the box. Could a courier pick it up from the warehouse ourselves?
  4. 4. Example: Last year my team had no budget for a proper event, so we had to think outside the box. Instead of booking a venue, we held it online and asked our customers to share short videos. It cost almost nothing, and more people took part than we expected.
