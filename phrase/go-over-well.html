go over well

IdiomLevel B2NeutralOpinions & honesty

To be received in a good way by other people.

Her joke didn't go over well at the meeting.

Tip: Use it with a subject that is an idea, event or message, and add an adverb like well, badly or surprisingly well: 'The new schedule went over badly.' Don't use it for people, as in 'I went over well'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow was the team dinner on Friday? Did people like the new restaurant?
KofiMostly, yes. The food was great, but the live music was way too loud.
OlenaOh no. I suggested that place because of the music.
KofiDon't worry. The idea of a dinner out went over well, and everyone chatted a lot anyway.
OlenaGood. Next time I'll pick somewhere quieter.

Tell me about a time you suggested an idea to a group of people. How did they react?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I suggested a weekly walking meeting to my team instead of sitting in the office. I was worried people would find it strange, but it went over well, and now we do it every Thursday. A few people even said it helped them think more clearly.

Answer key: go over well

  1. 1. A. People received it positively
  2. 2. went over
  3. 3. B. It went over well. The clients asked a lot of good questions.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I suggested a weekly walking meeting to my team instead of sitting in the office. I was worried people would find it strange, but it went over well, and now we do it every Thursday. A few people even said it helped them think more clearly.
