teacher's pet

IdiomLevel B1CasualPeople & relationships

A student the teacher likes best and treats better than the others.

Everyone laughed because Sam was the teacher's pet and always brought her an apple.

Tip: Say 'he's a teacher's pet' or 'she's the teacher's pet' about someone who gets special treatment, usually with a slightly critical or jealous tone, and use it outside school too, such as with a boss.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you see Diego left early again today?
KofiYes, and nobody said a word. The manager lets him do whatever he wants.
AmiraHe's a total teacher's pet. He laughs at every joke she makes.
KofiTrue, but he does a lot of good work too.
AmiraMaybe. I just wish she'd notice the rest of us once in a while.

Was there ever someone at school or work who seemed to get special treatment? Tell me about it.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Yes, at my first job there was a guy who was definitely the teacher's pet. Our manager gave him the best projects and never checked his mistakes. It annoyed me at the time, but I think he was just good at making her feel important.

Answer key: teacher's pet

  1. 1. B. Someone the manager likes best and treats better than the others
  2. 2. teacher's pet
  3. 3. A. Typical. He's the boss's teacher's pet, so he never gets in trouble.
  4. 4. Example: Yes, at my first job there was a guy who was definitely the teacher's pet. Our manager gave him the best projects and never checked his mistakes. It annoyed me at the time, but I think he was just good at making her feel important.
