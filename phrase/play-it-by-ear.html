play it by ear

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To decide what to do as things happen, without a fixed plan.

I don't have a plan for Friday. Let's just play it by ear.

Tip: Use it as 'play it by ear' or 'play things by ear', often after 'let's' or 'we'll'; it's about plans, not music, so don't use it for people ('play him by ear').

Hear it in conversation

AmiraAre we booking a restaurant for after the hike?
KofiI'd rather not. If the weather turns bad, we'll want to head straight home.
AmiraTrue. And some people might be too tired anyway.
KofiExactly. Let's play it by ear and find somewhere when we get down.
AmiraFine by me. I'll check which places are open on Sundays, just in case.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'play it by ear' mean?

2. Fill the gap

We don't know how long the meeting will run, so we'll have to and see if there's time for lunch afterwards.

3. Choose the best reply

Olena: Do you want to fix a time for the picnic, or just see what the weather does?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you travelled or went out without a fixed plan. How did it go?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last summer I spent a weekend in a new city with a friend, and we didn't book anything in advance. We decided to play it by ear, so each morning we chose what to do depending on the weather and how tired we were. It turned out to be one of the best trips I've had.

Answer key: play it by ear

  1. 1. D. Decide what to do later, depending on how things go
  2. 2. play it by ear
  3. 3. A. Let's play it by ear. If it's sunny on Saturday, we'll go.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer I spent a weekend in a new city with a friend, and we didn't book anything in advance. We decided to play it by ear, so each morning we chose what to do depending on the weather and how tired we were. It turned out to be one of the best trips I've had.
