chock full of

ExpressionLevel B2CasualEveryday life

Completely full, with a lot of things inside.

My suitcase is chock full of gifts for my family.

Tip: Use it after 'be' (or 'seem') and before 'of' + a plural or uncountable noun: 'chock full of ideas'. Don't say 'chock full with', and don't use it for a person's feelings.

Hear it in conversation

KofiDid you manage to find anything to eat in the fridge?
OlenaNot really. It's chock full of jars, but nothing I can make a meal from.
KofiHa, what kind of jars?
OlenaPickles, three kinds of mustard, and some jam from last year.
KofiLet's just order a pizza and sort it out tomorrow.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a place you know that is very busy or crowded. What is it like?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

My local market on Saturday mornings is chock full of people, so it's hard to move between the stalls. Everyone is looking for fresh fruit and cheap vegetables, and the noise is incredible. I still love going, though, because the atmosphere is so friendly.

Answer key: chock full of

  1. 1. C. Packed with a large amount of something
  2. 2. chock full of
  3. 3. B. It's lovely, and her bookshelves are chock full of novels in three languages.
  4. 4. Example: My local market on Saturday mornings is chock full of people, so it's hard to move between the stalls. Everyone is looking for fresh fruit and cheap vegetables, and the noise is incredible. I still love going, though, because the atmosphere is so friendly.
