the calm before the storm

IdiomLevel B2NeutralWork & study

A quiet time just before something busy or difficult begins.

The café is quiet now, but it's the calm before the storm. Lunch is coming.

Tip: Say 'it's the calm before the storm' about a quiet moment when you already know something busy or difficult is coming; don't use it for a peaceful time with nothing ahead.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaThe office is so peaceful this morning. I actually finished my coffee while it was hot.
KofiEnjoy it. This is the calm before the storm.
OlenaWhy? What's happening?
KofiThe new software goes live at noon, and the whole company will have questions.
OlenaRight. I'll finish my emails now, then.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when things were quiet just before a very busy or stressful period.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month, the week before my sister's wedding was strangely quiet. All the planning was done, so I thought, this is the calm before the storm. Sure enough, the day before the wedding, relatives arrived and everything became chaotic.

Answer key: the calm before the storm

  1. 1. C. A quiet time just before something busy or difficult starts
  2. 2. the calm before the storm
  3. 3. C. Yes, it's the calm before the storm. We should get ready now.
  4. 4. Example: Last month, the week before my sister's wedding was strangely quiet. All the planning was done, so I thought, this is the calm before the storm. Sure enough, the day before the wedding, relatives arrived and everything became chaotic.
