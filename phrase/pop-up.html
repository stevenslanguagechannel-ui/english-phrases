pop up

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To pop up is to appear suddenly, like a small window on a screen.

An ad keeps popping up on my screen.

Tip: Use 'pop up' without an object: things pop up (notifications, problems, ideas), and you can add 'on' or 'in' for the place, e.g. 'pop up on my phone'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you finish booking the flights?
KenAlmost, but the payment page froze, and then a weird message popped up.
AmiraWhat did it say?
KenSomething about my card, but it disappeared before I could read it.
AmiraTry again. Maybe use a different browser.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time something unexpected happened while you were using your phone or computer.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last week I was in the middle of an online video call with my manager when a loud advert suddenly popped up on my screen. I couldn't find the button to close it, so I felt really embarrassed. In the end, I had to restart my laptop.

Answer key: pop up

  1. 1. D. Appeared suddenly on the screen
  2. 2. popped up
  3. 3. B. Sorry, I was in a meeting, and I only saw your message when it popped up on my laptop just now.
  4. 4. Example: Last week I was in the middle of an online video call with my manager when a loud advert suddenly popped up on my screen. I couldn't find the button to close it, so I felt really embarrassed. In the end, I had to restart my laptop.
