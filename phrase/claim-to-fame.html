claim to fame

IdiomLevel B2CasualPeople & relationships

The one interesting or impressive thing a person or place is known for.

My claim to fame is that I once shook hands with a famous singer.

Tip: Use it with 'my/his/her/its claim to fame is...' or 'X's claim to fame is that...'; it's usually light and a bit playful, so don't use it for serious achievements.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaSo what's special about your hometown, Kofi?
KofiHonestly, not much. Our only claim to fame is a giant statue of a pineapple.
OlenaA pineapple? Seriously?
KofiSeriously. People drive for hours just to take a photo next to it.
OlenaOkay, now I want to visit.

Show an example answer

My hometown's main claim to fame is its night market, which is popular with visitors from all over the region. Other than that, it's a pretty quiet place. My cousin also has a small claim to fame, because he once appeared on local TV for finding a lost dog.
