the grass is always greener on the other side

IdiomLevel B2CasualFeelings & reactions

Other people's lives or things always look better than your own.

I want her job, but the grass is always greener on the other side.

Tip: Say it when someone is tempted by, or envies, something they don't have; it often warns that the other option may not be as good as it looks, and it is usually followed by a comment about the problems you can't see.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaKofi, you work from home now, right? I'm so jealous. My commute is an hour each way.
KofiIt's not as great as it sounds. I miss the office, and I never switch off.
OlenaReally? I'd happily swap. No traffic, no early alarm.
KofiMaybe. But I think the grass is always greener on the other side, you know?
OlenaOkay, fair. I do like chatting with my team over lunch.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'the grass is always greener on the other side' mean?

2. Fill the gap

She keeps changing careers because she thinks , but each new job has the same kind of problems.

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: I'm thinking of moving to another city. Everyone there seems so happy and relaxed!

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you wanted something someone else had, but it turned out to be different from what you expected.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

A few years ago I envied my friend's flexible freelance life. When I tried it for a month, I realised the money was unpredictable and I felt isolated. It really is true that the grass is always greener on the other side.

Answer key: the grass is always greener on the other side

  1. 1. D. People tend to think other people's situations are better than their own, even when they aren't.
  2. 2. the grass is always greener on the other side
  3. 3. B. Maybe, but remember that the grass is always greener on the other side. Every city has its stress.
  4. 4. Example: A few years ago I envied my friend's flexible freelance life. When I tried it for a month, I realised the money was unpredictable and I felt isolated. It really is true that the grass is always greener on the other side.
