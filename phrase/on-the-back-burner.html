on the back burner

IdiomLevel B2NeutralTime & effort

Something is not urgent, so you will deal with it later.

The website project is on the back burner until next month.

Tip: Use it as 'put something on the back burner' or 'something is on the back burner'; it works for plans, projects and ideas, but not for people.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you still planning to repaint the spare room this month?
KofiNot really. The kitchen tap is leaking and the car needs a service, so the painting is on the back burner.
OlenaThat makes sense. Fix the tap first, before it gets worse.
KofiExactly. I'll look at paint colours again in the spring.
OlenaGood idea. The room isn't going anywhere.

2. Fill the gap

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: Did you ever start that online photography course you were talking about?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a goal or project you have postponed. Why did you decide to wait?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I've wanted to learn to drive for a while, but I've put it on the back burner this year. I'm studying for exams and the lessons are quite expensive, so it just isn't a priority right now. I'll probably start again once things calm down.

Answer key: on the back burner

  1. 1. B. Kofi has decided it is not important now and will do it later
  2. 2. on the back burner
  3. 3. C. Not yet. With work so busy, it's on the back burner at the moment.
  4. 4. Example: I've wanted to learn to drive for a while, but I've put it on the back burner this year. I'm studying for exams and the lessons are quite expensive, so it just isn't a priority right now. I'll probably start again once things calm down.
