cut off

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To be disconnected while you are on a call.

Sorry, I got cut off. My signal is bad.

Tip: Use it for calls and connections, often in the passive: 'I got cut off' or 'We were cut off'; don't confuse it with 'cut someone off', which means to interrupt them or stop contact.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraKen, I tried to ring you twenty minutes ago. What happened?
KenWe were halfway through the booking when we got cut off, and then my phone had no signal at all.
AmiraOh no. Did the hotel say your room was confirmed?
KenI'm not sure. I'll call them back from the café where the Wi-Fi works.
AmiraGood idea. Ask them to send you an email this time.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when technology didn't work well when you needed it. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I had an important online interview, and my internet was very slow. About ten minutes in, I got cut off and I had to join the call again from my phone. It was stressful, but the interviewer was kind about it, and we finished the conversation without any more problems.

Answer key: cut off

  1. 1. B. The call suddenly ended before they had finished talking
  2. 2. cut off
  3. 3. A. Sorry, we got cut off. My battery died in the middle of the call.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I had an important online interview, and my internet was very slow. About ten minutes in, I got cut off and I had to join the call again from my phone. It was stressful, but the interviewer was kind about it, and we finished the conversation without any more problems.
