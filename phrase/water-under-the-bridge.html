water under the bridge

IdiomLevel B2CasualPeople & relationships

Something unpleasant that happened in the past and is no longer important.

We argued last year, but that's water under the bridge now.

Tip: Use it after a conflict or mistake to say it no longer matters; it's often said as 'that's water under the bridge' or 'it's all water under the bridge now'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraKofi, I'm sorry again about missing your moving day. I felt awful.
KofiAmira, honestly, don't worry. It's water under the bridge.
AmiraReally? You were pretty annoyed at the time.
KofiI was, but we got everything done in the end.
AmiraThanks. Let me bring dinner to the new flat on Friday.
KofiDeal. And bring a big appetite.

Answer key: water under the bridge

  1. 1. C. A past problem that no longer matters
  2. 2. water under the bridge
  3. 3. A. Thanks, but it's water under the bridge. Let's move on.
