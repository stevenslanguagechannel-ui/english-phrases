go off

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To make a loud sound, like an alarm or a phone.

My alarm went off at six, but I stayed in bed.

Tip: Use 'go off' for alarms, phones, smoke detectors and car alarms; it's intransitive, so you can't say 'go off the alarm' (use 'set off' for that).

Hear it in conversation

KofiYou look exhausted, Amira. Did you not sleep well?
AmiraNot really. The smoke alarm in the hallway went off at three in the morning.
KofiWas there a fire?
AmiraNo, the battery was just low. It kept beeping until I found a ladder.
KofiUgh, that's the worst. Did you change it?
AmiraYes, first thing this morning. Never again.
