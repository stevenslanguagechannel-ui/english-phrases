be down (website or network)

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralProblems & solutions

If a website or network is down, it is not working right now.

I can't pay the bill. The website is down.

Tip: Use it with a website, app or network as the subject: 'The server is down' or 'Is the Wi-Fi down?'. Don't use it for a person or a device like a phone, and remember it means the service isn't working, not that it is slow.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraKen, did you manage to book our train tickets?
KenNot yet. I tried three times this morning.
AmiraWhat happens when you open the page?
KenNothing loads. I think the whole booking site is down.
AmiraOkay, let's try again after lunch. The fares might change, though.
KenI know. I'll check every half hour.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "the whole booking site is down" mean?

2. Fill the gap

I couldn't join the online class because the school's server was for most of the morning.

3. Choose the best reply

Diego: I tried to check my bank balance, but the app wouldn't open at all.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when a website or app didn't work when you needed it. What did you do?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I tried to submit a form online on the deadline day, but the website was down. I waited about an hour, then I called the office and they gave me an extra day. Now I never leave things until the last minute.

Answer key: be down (website or network)

  1. 1. C. The site is not working at the moment, so Ken can't use it.
  2. 2. down
  3. 3. A. Maybe their system is down. Try again in a few minutes.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I tried to submit a form online on the deadline day, but the website was down. I waited about an hour, then I called the office and they gave me an extra day. Now I never leave things until the last minute.
