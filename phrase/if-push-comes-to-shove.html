if push comes to shove

IdiomLevel B2NeutralProblems & solutions

If the situation becomes really difficult and you must act.

If push comes to shove, I'll work on Saturday to finish the report.

Tip: Start with 'If push comes to shove,' then say what you would do as a last resort; it is not used for ordinary plans.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaOur flight lands at midnight. Is there any way to get to the hotel?
KofiThe trains stop at eleven, so we can't count on them.
OlenaWhat if the taxi queue is huge?
KofiI'll book a shuttle online now. And if push comes to shove, we can pay extra for a ride-share.
OlenaGood idea. I'd rather not wait outside at that hour.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you had a plan but also needed a backup plan.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I was travelling to a friend's wedding, and my train was often delayed. I planned to take the early train, but if push comes to shove, I decided I would drive there instead. In the end the train was late, so I borrowed my brother's car and arrived just in time.

Answer key: if push comes to shove

  1. 1. C. If nothing else works and we have to choose a last option
  2. 2. if push comes to shove
  3. 3. B. If push comes to shove, I can do the layout myself tonight.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I was travelling to a friend's wedding, and my train was often delayed. I planned to take the early train, but if push comes to shove, I decided I would drive there instead. In the end the train was late, so I borrowed my brother's car and arrived just in time.
