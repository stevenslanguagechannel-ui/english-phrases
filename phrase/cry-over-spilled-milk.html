cry over spilled milk

IdiomLevel B1CasualFeelings & reactions

To be upset about something bad that already happened and can't be changed.

The cake is ruined, but there's no use crying over spilled milk. Let's make another one.

Tip: Use it in the pattern 'no use crying over spilled milk' or 'there's no point crying over spilled milk' to comfort someone, but avoid saying it to a person who is still very upset, because it can sound dismissive.

Hear it in conversation

KofiI can't believe I missed the 8:15 train. Now I'll be late for the whole workshop.
AmiraDid you check when the next one leaves?
Kofi8:40. I keep thinking I should have left the house ten minutes earlier.
AmiraWell, it's too late now. There's no point crying over spilled milk.
KofiYou're right. I'll message the organiser and grab a coffee at the station.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'crying over spilled milk' mean?

2. Fill the gap

I failed my driving test by one mark, but being angry about it is just . I'll book another one tomorrow.

3. Choose the best reply

Ken: I deleted the wrong file and now I can't get the old version back. I keep going over it in my head.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you made a mistake and had to move on from it. How did you handle it?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I booked the wrong dates for a trip and lost some money on the hotel. I was annoyed for a day, but then I told myself there was no use crying over spilled milk, so I rebooked and learned to double-check the calendar next time.

Answer key: cry over spilled milk

  1. 1. D. Feeling sad about a mistake that can't be undone
  2. 2. crying over spilled milk
  3. 3. A. Try not to cry over spilled milk. Let's just rewrite it together this afternoon.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I booked the wrong dates for a trip and lost some money on the hotel. I was annoyed for a day, but then I told myself there was no use crying over spilled milk, so I rebooked and learned to double-check the calendar next time.
