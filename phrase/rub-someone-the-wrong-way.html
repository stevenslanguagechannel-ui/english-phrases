rub someone the wrong way

IdiomLevel B2CasualPeople & relationships

If something rubs you the wrong way, it annoys you or feels not right, often without a clear reason.

He's very nice, but his loud laugh rubs me the wrong way.

Tip: Use it with a person as the object (rub me / him / her the wrong way); the thing that annoys you is the subject, and you often can't say exactly why.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow was the meeting with the new client yesterday?
KofiFine on paper. They agreed to everything, but something about the manager rubbed me the wrong way.
OlenaReally? What did he do?
KofiNothing specific. He was polite, he just seemed too smooth, you know?
OlenaHmm. Maybe give him another chance at the next call.
KofiYeah, I will. I might be wrong about him.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a person or a habit that you find slightly annoying, even though you can't fully explain why.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

There's a colleague at my office who's perfectly nice, but the way he says "No worries" after every mistake really rubs me the wrong way. I know he's trying to be kind, so I never say anything. It's just one of those small things that stays in my head.

Answer key: rub someone the wrong way

  1. 1. B. He made me feel uneasy or irritated, though I can't say exactly why
  2. 2. rubs me the wrong way
  3. 3. B. He is friendly, but his habit of correcting everyone rubs me the wrong way.
  4. 4. Example: There's a colleague at my office who's perfectly nice, but the way he says "No worries" after every mistake really rubs me the wrong way. I know he's trying to be kind, so I never say anything. It's just one of those small things that stays in my head.
