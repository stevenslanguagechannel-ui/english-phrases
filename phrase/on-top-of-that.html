on top of that

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralProblems & solutions

In addition to something already mentioned, often something else annoying or surprising.

The train was late, and on top of that, it started to rain.

Tip: Use it to add one more point to a list of things, usually problems; it often starts a new clause, followed by a comma, and you can also say 'on top of this' or 'on top of everything'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraHow was your move on Saturday?
DiegoHonestly, a mess. The van arrived two hours late.
AmiraOh no. Was everything okay in the end?
DiegoEventually. But the lift in the new building was broken, and on top of that, I had to carry the sofa up six floors.
AmiraSix floors? You must have been exhausted!
DiegoI could hardly walk on Sunday.

Tell me about a day when everything seemed to go wrong. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last winter I had a terrible day. I woke up late and missed my train, and on top of that, it was pouring with rain and I had no umbrella. I arrived at work soaking wet, but at least my colleagues made me a hot drink.

Answer key: on top of that

  1. 1. D. Besides that, as another problem
  2. 2. on top of that
  3. 3. A. Oh no! And on top of that, you probably had to present the next day.
  4. 4. Example: Last winter I had a terrible day. I woke up late and missed my train, and on top of that, it was pouring with rain and I had no umbrella. I arrived at work soaking wet, but at least my colleagues made me a hot drink.
