get around to

Phrasal verbLevel B2

To finally do something you meant to do for a long time.

I finally got around to cleaning the garage this weekend.

Get around to + -ing means to finally do something you planned for a long time. We often use it with 'never' or 'finally'.

Your turn
What have you still not got around to doing?
