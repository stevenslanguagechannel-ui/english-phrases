breathing down your neck

IdiomLevel B2CasualWork & study

Watching someone very closely, so they feel pressure.

I can't focus when my boss is breathing down my neck.

Tip: Use it with a person or group as the subject: 'someone is breathing down my neck' (usually while you are working on something), and don't use it for a literal breath.

Hear it in conversation

MeiAre you still working on the report? It's nearly eight.
DiegoYeah, I'm stuck on the last section. The client keeps emailing me for updates.
MeiEvery hour?
DiegoPretty much. With them breathing down my neck, I can't think straight.
MeiTurn off your notifications for an hour. You'll finish faster.

Tell me about a time when someone put pressure on you while you were trying to get something done.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I was finishing a big project at university, and my supervisor was breathing down my neck about the deadline. She asked for updates almost every day, which made me quite stressed. In the end I finished on time, but I would have enjoyed the work more with a bit of space.

Answer key: breathing down your neck

  1. 1. B. Checking on his work all the time, which makes him feel pressure
  2. 2. breathing down my neck
  3. 3. A. I know, she's been breathing down your neck all week, hasn't she?
  4. 4. Example: Last year I was finishing a big project at university, and my supervisor was breathing down my neck about the deadline. She asked for updates almost every day, which made me quite stressed. In the end I finished on time, but I would have enjoyed the work more with a bit of space.
