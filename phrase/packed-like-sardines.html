packed like sardines

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

If a place is packed like sardines, it is so full that people have almost no space.

The train was packed like sardines, so I stood the whole way.

Tip: Use 'be packed like sardines' (or 'be crammed in like sardines') for crowded trains, buses, lifts and rooms; it is a vivid exaggeration, so avoid it in formal writing.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraHow was the concert last night, Kofi?
KofiThe music was great, but the hall was so small. We were packed like sardines.
AmiraOh no. Could you even see the stage?
KofiOnly when the tall guy in front of me moved his head.
AmiraI'd have left early just to get some air.
KofiWe nearly did. The walk back to the station was the best part!

  1. 1. C. The place was so crowded that people had almost no room to move
  2. 2. packed like sardines
  3. 3. A. Terrible. We were packed like sardines the whole way.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer I went to a street market on a holiday weekend, and it was packed like sardines. I could hardly move, and I kept losing my friends in the crowd. In the end we bought a few snacks and left early.
