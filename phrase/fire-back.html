fire back

Phrasal verbLevel B2NeutralFeelings & reactions

To answer quickly and sharply, usually when you disagree or feel criticised.

When I criticised his plan, he fired back with a long email.

Tip: Use 'fire back' with 'with' + what was said, or a quoted reply: 'She fired back with a quick message.' It suggests the reply was fast and a bit sharp, so don't use it for a calm, thoughtful answer.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you see Ken's message in the group chat? He said our team always hands in work late.
DiegoI did. Olena was really annoyed by it.
AmiraDid she answer him?
DiegoYes, within a minute. She fired back that his own reports are never ready on time.
AmiraOh no. I hope they sort it out before Monday's meeting.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when someone criticised you or disagreed with you. How did you react?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

A colleague once said my report was messy, in front of the whole team. I was embarrassed, so I fired back that he hadn't even read it properly. Looking back, I wish I had waited a few minutes before answering.

Answer key: fire back

  1. 1. D. Replied quickly and sharply to the criticism
  2. 2. fired back
  3. 3. B. Did you fire back at him, or did you stay calm?
  4. 4. Example: A colleague once said my report was messy, in front of the whole team. I was embarrassed, so I fired back that he hadn't even read it properly. Looking back, I wish I had waited a few minutes before answering.
