count me in

ExpressionLevel B1CasualPeople & relationships

A friendly way to say you want to join an activity.

You're going bowling on Friday? Count me in!

Tip: Say 'Count me in' when an invitation is open and you want to join; it's a short reply, so you don't add a verb after it. To say no politely, use 'Count me out'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraOlena and I are planning a picnic by the river on Sunday.
KofiNice idea! Is it just the two of you?
AmiraNo, anyone can come. We're meeting at noon, and everyone brings something to share.
KofiCount me in. I'll make my pasta salad.
OlenaPerfect! We'll bring the blankets and some games.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when friends or colleagues invited you to join something and you said yes. What was it, and how did it go?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month my colleagues were organising a charity run, and when they asked who wanted to take part, I said, "Count me in!" I'd never run 5 kilometres before, so we trained together after work. It was hard, but we all finished, and I'm really glad I said yes.

Answer key: count me in

  1. 1. D. I want to join you and take part.
  2. 2. count you in
  3. 3. C. Count me in! What time are we meeting?
