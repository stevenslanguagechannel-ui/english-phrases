for lack of a better way of putting it

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralOpinions & honesty

Said when you can't find the perfect words, so you use the closest ones.

He's, for lack of a better way of putting it, a very loud neighbour.

Tip: Put it in the middle of a sentence, usually before a description you know isn't perfect; it's often set off with commas, and don't use it when you actually have the exact word.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraSo, how was the new manager's first team meeting?
KenInteresting. She's, for lack of a better way of putting it, very hands-on.
AmiraHands-on? You mean she checks everything?
KenPretty much. She asked to see our drafts before we'd even finished them.
AmiraHmm. That might get tiring after a while.
KenIt might. But she does seem to care about the work.

Describe a place you have visited that was very different from what you expected.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I visited a small town last year that was, for lack of a better way of putting it, much quieter than I'd imagined. I'd expected cafés and shops, but by six o'clock almost everything was closed. In the end I actually enjoyed the peace.

Answer key: for lack of a better way of putting it

  1. 1. D. I can't think of a more exact way to say this, so this is close enough.
  2. 2. for lack of a better way of putting it
  3. 3. B. Well, for lack of a better way of putting it, he's very tidy. Almost too tidy!
  4. 4. Example: I visited a small town last year that was, for lack of a better way of putting it, much quieter than I'd imagined. I'd expected cafés and shops, but by six o'clock almost everything was closed. In the end I actually enjoyed the peace.
