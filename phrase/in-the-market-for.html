in the market for

IdiomLevel B2NeutralEveryday life

Interested in buying a particular thing.

I'm in the market for a used bicycle.

Tip: Use 'be in the market for + noun' for things you plan to buy, usually bigger items like a car, laptop or flat; don't use it for things you already own or for services you just want to use.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaIs that your old laptop? It sounds like it's about to take off.
KofiI know, the fan is so loud. It's six years old and the battery barely lasts an hour.
OlenaSo are you going to replace it?
KofiYes, I'm in the market for something lighter, ideally under a thousand.
OlenaMy cousin works in an electronics shop. I'll ask if there are any sales coming up.
KofiThat would be great, thanks.

Show an example answer

Yes, I'm in the market for a new desk chair because my back hurts after long days of studying. I've been trying a few in shops, but I haven't found a comfortable one at a good price yet.

Answer key: in the market for

  1. 1. D. Thinking about buying something
  2. 2. in the market for
  3. 3. B. Yes, I'm in the market for a new one, so I'm comparing a few models.
  4. 4. Example: Yes, I'm in the market for a new desk chair because my back hurts after long days of studying. I've been trying a few in shops, but I haven't found a comfortable one at a good price yet.
