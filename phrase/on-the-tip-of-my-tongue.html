on the tip of my tongue

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

You almost remember a word or name, but you can't say it yet.

His name is on the tip of my tongue, but I can't remember it.

Tip: Say 'It's on the tip of my tongue' or 'The word is on the tip of my tongue' when you nearly remember something; don't use it for things you have forgotten completely.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraWho was that actor in the film we watched last weekend? The one with the funny laugh.
KofiOh, I know who you mean. It's on the tip of my tongue.
AmiraWas he in that detective series too?
KofiYes! Hang on... don't tell me. It starts with a D.
AmiraDan? Daniel? Just search for it on your phone.
KofiDominic! Found it. Dominic Hayes.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does 'on the tip of my tongue' mean?

2. Fill the gap

The name of that café is , but I just can't get it.

3. Choose the best reply

Ken: Do you remember what that song was called? The one we heard in the taxi.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you couldn't remember someone's name when you met them.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

At a work event last month, I saw a colleague from my old office and her name was on the tip of my tongue. I could remember her job and even her dog's name, but not her name! In the end I asked a friend quietly, and I felt a bit embarrassed.

Answer key: on the tip of my tongue

  1. 1. C. Kofi almost remembers the actor's name but can't say it yet.
  2. 2. on the tip of my tongue
  3. 3. A. Hmm, it's on the tip of my tongue. Give me a second.
  4. 4. Example: At a work event last month, I saw a colleague from my old office and her name was on the tip of my tongue. I could remember her job and even her dog's name, but not her name! In the end I asked a friend quietly, and I felt a bit embarrassed.
