You would think (that)...

ExpressionLevel B2

Used when you expect something to be true or happen, but in reality it doesn't.

You would think the bus would be on time in the rain, but it was late again.

Use 'You would think...' when reality is the opposite of what you expect. It often shows mild surprise or frustration.
