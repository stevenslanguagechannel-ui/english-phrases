You would think (that)...

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralOpinions & honesty

Used when you expect something to be true or happen, but in reality it doesn't.

You would think the bus would be on time in the rain, but it was late again.

Tip: Follow it with a clause describing the logical expectation, then use 'but' or 'yet' to say what really happened; it often signals mild frustration or surprise.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid you get an answer from the landlord about the broken heater?
KofiNot yet. I've emailed him three times this week.
OlenaThree times? You would think he'd reply to at least one of them.
KofiI know. It's been freezing in the flat since Monday.
OlenaMaybe try calling him. People often answer the phone faster than email.
KofiGood idea. I'll ring him after lunch.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "You would think he'd reply to at least one of them" mean?

2. Fill the gap

a company that size would have a working website, but I couldn't even log in to book my appointment.

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: I've been waiting forty minutes at the pharmacy, and they still haven't called my name.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when something didn't go the way you expected, even though it should have been easy.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I tried to renew my passport online. You would think a government website would make it simple, but the form kept crashing, and in the end I had to go in person. It took me half a day instead of ten minutes.

Answer key: You would think (that)...

  1. 1. B. Olena expects the landlord to reply, but he hasn't, and that surprises or annoys her.
  2. 2. You would think
  3. 3. A. You would think they'd be quicker, since it's not even busy today.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I tried to renew my passport online. You would think a government website would make it simple, but the form kept crashing, and in the end I had to go in person. It took me half a day instead of ten minutes.
