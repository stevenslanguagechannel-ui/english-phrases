let your hair down

IdiomLevel B2CasualFeelings & reactions

To relax and have fun without worrying about being serious.

It's Friday! Let your hair down and enjoy the party.

Tip: Use it as 'let your hair down' (or 'let her/his hair down') for someone who finally relaxes after being serious or busy; it's friendly, so save it for casual or neutral situations.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaYou were so quiet at the team dinner last night, Kofi.
KofiI know. I'm always worried about saying the wrong thing in front of the managers.
OlenaBut the whole project is finished now. Nobody expects you to be serious all evening.
KofiTrue. Maybe at the leaving party on Friday I'll finally let my hair down.
OlenaGood idea! I'll save you a seat at the table with the funniest people.

2. Fill the gap

3. Choose the best reply

Diego: You've been working non-stop since January. What are you doing for your holiday?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you were able to relax completely after a stressful period. What did you do?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

After my final exams last year, I was exhausted, so I went away with a few friends for the weekend. We didn't plan anything serious, we just cooked, played games and talked for hours. It was the first time in months that I could really let my hair down.

Answer key: let your hair down

  1. 1. B. Relax and enjoy myself without worrying about being serious
  2. 2. let her hair down
  3. 3. A. Nothing planned except a beach, good food and finally letting my hair down.
  4. 4. Example: After my final exams last year, I was exhausted, so I went away with a few friends for the weekend. We didn't plan anything serious, we just cooked, played games and talked for hours. It was the first time in months that I could really let my hair down.
