hand in

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralWork & study

To give your work to a teacher or boss.

Please hand in your homework before you leave.

Tip: Use 'hand in' + the thing (hand in a report) or 'hand it in' with a pronoun, never 'hand in it'; it is about giving finished work to someone in charge.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you coming to the café tonight, Ken?
KenI can't. I have to hand in my project report by nine tomorrow morning.
OlenaOh no. How much is left?
KenJust the conclusion and the references. I'll finish it by midnight.
OlenaGood luck. Send me a message when it's done.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you had to finish something before a deadline.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I had a big report at work, and I left it too late. I stayed up until two in the morning to finish it, and I hand it in just before the office opened. Now I always start earlier so I'm not so stressed.

Answer key: hand in

  1. 1. C. Give your finished work to the person who asked for it
  2. 2. hand in
  3. 3. C. Yes, I handed it in at the front desk this morning.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I had a big report at work, and I left it too late. I stayed up until two in the morning to finish it, and I hand it in just before the office opened. Now I always start earlier so I'm not so stressed.
