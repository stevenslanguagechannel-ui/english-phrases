zone out

Phrasal verbLevel B2CasualWork & study

To stop paying attention and start thinking about nothing in particular.

Sorry, I zoned out during the lecture. Can you repeat that?

Tip: Use it for your own attention drifting, as in 'I zoned out' or 'she zones out in meetings'; it's not something you do on purpose, so avoid 'I zoned out the noise'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow was the training session this morning?
KofiHonestly, I zoned out after the first twenty minutes. It was all slides.
OlenaOh no. Did you miss anything important?
KofiProbably the part about the new booking system. I'll ask Mei for her notes.
OlenaGood idea. She always writes everything down.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "zoned out" mean?

2. Fill the gap

I was so tired on the train that I completely and missed my stop.

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: You've been staring at that screen for ages. Are you okay?

4. Your turn: speak

Describe a time when you found it hard to concentrate. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last week I had a really long online meeting after lunch, and I completely zoned out. I only realised when someone asked me a question and I had no idea what they'd been talking about. I had to ask them to repeat it, which was a bit embarrassing.

Answer key: zone out

  1. 1. C. Stopped paying attention and let his mind drift
  2. 2. zoned out
  3. 3. B. Sorry, I zoned out for a minute. I was thinking about nothing, really.
  4. 4. Example: Last week I had a really long online meeting after lunch, and I completely zoned out. I only realised when someone asked me a question and I had no idea what they'd been talking about. I had to ask them to repeat it, which was a bit embarrassing.
