the life of the party

IdiomLevel B2CasualPeople & relationships

A person who is full of fun and makes a party exciting.

Diego is always the life of the party. He makes everyone laugh.

Tip: Use it with 'be' or 'become' ('She's the life of the party'); it describes someone's energy at a social event, so it sounds odd for a quiet person or for a work meeting.

Hear it in conversation

MeiAre you coming to Olena's housewarming on Saturday?
KenI think so. I don't know many people there, though.
MeiDon't worry. Kofi will be there, and he's always the life of the party.
KenOh, good. Then I won't have to start any conversations myself.
MeiExactly. Just stand near him and you'll be laughing in five minutes.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a person you know who is great at social events. What are they like?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

My friend Ken is definitely the life of the party. He walks into a room and starts chatting with everyone, and he always knows how to make shy people feel comfortable. I'm usually quieter, so I'm glad when he's there.

Answer key: the life of the party

  1. 1. B. Someone who is cheerful and sociable and makes a gathering fun for everyone
  2. 2. the life of the party
  3. 3. C. Yes! My cousin was the life of the party, and he got everyone dancing.
  4. 4. Example: My friend Ken is definitely the life of the party. He walks into a room and starts chatting with everyone, and he always knows how to make shy people feel comfortable. I'm usually quieter, so I'm glad when he's there.
