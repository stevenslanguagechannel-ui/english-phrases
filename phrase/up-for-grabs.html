up for grabs

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

Available for anyone to take or win.

There's one ticket left, so it's up for grabs. Who wants it?

Tip: Use it for things anyone can claim, like jobs, prizes, seats or leftover food; it's usually 'be up for grabs' or 'is/are up for grabs', and it sounds odd for people or formal contracts.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaAre you taking the desk by the window? You've been sitting there all week.
KofiNo, it's not mine. Mei moved to the fourth floor yesterday.
OlenaSo nobody has claimed it yet?
KofiNot that I know of. I think it's up for grabs until the manager decides.
OlenaGreat, I'll move my laptop over now before someone else does.
KofiGood idea. That spot gets the best light.

Show an example answer

Last year my company announced that a place on a training course in another city was still up for grabs. Nobody on my team had applied yet, so I wrote to my manager straight away. I got the place, and it helped me a lot at work.

Answer key: up for grabs

  1. 1. C. Available for anyone who wants to take it
  2. 2. up for grabs
  3. 3. C. Not yet, so it's up for grabs. Help yourself.
  4. 4. Example: Last year my company announced that a place on a training course in another city was still up for grabs. Nobody on my team had applied yet, so I wrote to my manager straight away. I got the place, and it helped me a lot at work.
