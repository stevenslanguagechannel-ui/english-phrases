or else

ExpressionLevel B1CasualProblems & solutions

Used to say what bad thing will happen if you don't do something.

Leave now, or else you'll miss your flight.

Tip: Put 'or else' after a command or warning, then say the result: 'Do X, or else Y will happen.' It can sound like a threat if you say it firmly, so use a light tone with friends.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraHave you sent the group photos to everyone yet?
KenNot yet. I'll do it after dinner.
AmiraPlease do it tonight. Upload them today, or else the shared link will expire.
KenReally? I thought it lasted a week.
AmiraIt's only seven days from when I made it, and that was last Thursday.
KenOkay, okay. I'm opening my laptop now.

Tell me about a time you had to hurry to do something. What would have happened if you hadn't?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I had to send an important form before noon, or else I would have lost my place on a course. I left the house early and finished it on my phone on the train. I was really relieved when I got the confirmation email.

Answer key: or else

  1. 1. A. Otherwise, something bad will happen
  2. 2. or else
  3. 3. A. You should go, or else your manager might think you're not interested.
  4. 4. Example: Last month I had to send an important form before noon, or else I would have lost my place on a course. I left the house early and finished it on my phone on the train. I was really relieved when I got the confirmation email.
