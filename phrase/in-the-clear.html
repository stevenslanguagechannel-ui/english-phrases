in the clear

IdiomLevel B2NeutralProblems & solutions

Safe from danger, trouble or blame.

The test results came back, so we're in the clear.

Tip: Usually follows 'be' or 'get' (we're in the clear, you're in the clear); say it when a risk has passed, but not while the problem is still possible.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid the landlord say anything about the leak in the bathroom?
KofiYes, he sent a plumber this morning. The pipe is fixed and the ceiling is dry.
OlenaGreat. So we won't have to pay for the damage?
KofiNo, the insurance report says it was an old pipe. We're in the clear.
OlenaThat's a relief. I was dreading that email.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "in the clear" mean?

2. Fill the gap

The manager checked the numbers twice, and now Amira knows she is after the mistake in the report.

3. Choose the best reply

Ken: I was afraid I'd be blamed for the missed deadline, but the client says it wasn't my fault.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you were worried about getting into trouble, but it turned out fine.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Once I lost a library book and was sure I'd have to pay a large fine. Then I found it under my bed on the last day, and the librarian said it was fine. I was so relieved to be in the clear.

Answer key: in the clear

  1. 1. A. They are free from the risk of trouble or blame.
  2. 2. in the clear
  3. 3. A. Good news. Then you're in the clear.
  4. 4. Example: Once I lost a library book and was sure I'd have to pay a large fine. Then I found it under my bed on the last day, and the librarian said it was fine. I was so relieved to be in the clear.
