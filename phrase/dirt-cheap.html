dirt cheap

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

Extremely cheap, much less than you would expect.

I bought this jacket for five dollars. It was dirt cheap!

Tip: Use it after 'was/were/is' or 'sell/get/buy something': 'The tickets were dirt cheap.' Don't use it for people (not 'He's dirt cheap'), where it can sound like an insult about character.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaKofi, where did you get that desk lamp? It looks great on your desk.
KofiAt the market near my flat. The seller wanted four euros for it.
OlenaFour euros? Honestly, that's dirt cheap. In the shops here, a lamp like that costs thirty.
KofiI know! I almost bought two, in case this one breaks.
OlenaGood idea. Can you show me where the stall is on Saturday?
KofiSure, I'll message you the address.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about something you bought recently that was a really good deal.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I bought a second-hand bicycle last month, and it was dirt cheap. The owner was moving abroad and just wanted it gone, so I paid about a quarter of the normal price. It's in great condition, and I use it to get to work every day.

Answer key: dirt cheap

  1. 1. C. Very low in price, much less than expected
  2. 2. dirt cheap
  3. 3. A. Wow, that's dirt cheap! How did you find them?
  4. 4. Example: I bought a second-hand bicycle last month, and it was dirt cheap. The owner was moving abroad and just wanted it gone, so I paid about a quarter of the normal price. It's in great condition, and I use it to get to work every day.
