a no-brainer

IdiomLevel B1CasualProblems & solutions

A decision that is so easy you don't need to think about it.

Taking the free ticket was a no-brainer.

Tip: Use it as a noun after 'a' or 'the' ("It was a no-brainer", "The choice is a no-brainer"); say it about a choice, not about a person, because calling someone a no-brainer sounds rude.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you decide about the apartment near the station?
KofiYes! It's ten minutes from work, and the rent is lower than what I pay now.
AmiraSo you're taking it?
KofiOf course. Once I saw the price, it was a no-brainer.
AmiraNice. When do you move in?
KofiNext month. I'm already packing boxes.

2. Fill the gap

3. Choose the best reply

Olena: They're offering you the job, and it pays more and is closer to home. What are you going to do?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a decision you made recently that was very easy. Why was it so easy?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month my friend invited me to share a taxi to the airport, and it was a no-brainer. It cost half the price and saved me an hour of waiting for the bus. I said yes right away.

Answer key: a no-brainer

  1. 1. A. A choice that is very easy to make
  2. 2. no-brainer
  3. 3. B. Accept it. Honestly, it's a no-brainer.
  4. 4. Example: Last month my friend invited me to share a taxi to the airport, and it was a no-brainer. It cost half the price and saved me an hour of waiting for the bus. I said yes right away.
