herding cats

IdiomLevel B2CasualWork & study

Trying to control or organise a group that will not cooperate or stay together.

Getting ten kids to put on their shoes at once is like herding cats.

Tip: Use 'like herding cats' or 'It's (like) herding cats' for any job where the people or things involved keep going their own way; it's about the difficulty of organising, not about cats.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow did the group project meeting go?
KofiHonestly, I'm exhausted. Five of us, five different ideas about the deadline.
OlenaDid you at least agree on who does what?
KofiNot even close. Two people left early and one kept checking his phone. Trying to get everyone on the same page was herding cats.
OlenaMaybe send a short written plan tonight? Then nobody can say they forgot.
KofiGood idea. I'll do that after dinner.

3. Choose the best reply

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you had to organise a group of people. How did it go?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I organised a leaving party for a colleague, and it was like herding cats. Everyone replied at different times and nobody agreed on a date. In the end I made a decision myself and sent one clear message, and that finally worked.

Answer key: herding cats

  1. 1. B. Trying to organise people who won't cooperate or stay focused
  2. 2. herding cats
  3. 3. C. Sounds like herding cats. Maybe just pick a time and send an invite?
  4. 4. Example: Last year I organised a leaving party for a colleague, and it was like herding cats. Everyone replied at different times and nobody agreed on a date. In the end I made a decision myself and sent one clear message, and that finally worked.
