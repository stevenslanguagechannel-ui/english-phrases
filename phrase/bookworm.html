bookworm

IdiomLevel B1CasualPeople & relationships

A person who loves reading and reads a lot.

My sister is a real bookworm. She reads every night.

Tip: Use it as a noun after 'a' or 'a real': 'He's a bookworm.' It's friendly and describes a person's habit, so it works for kids, friends and family; don't use it as a verb.

Hear it in conversation

KofiI need a birthday present for my cousin. Any ideas?
AmiraWhat's she into?
KofiNot much sport or gaming. She's a bookworm, always carrying a novel around.
AmiraThen a gift card for a bookshop is perfect. She can pick her own.
KofiGood idea. I'll get her one this afternoon.
