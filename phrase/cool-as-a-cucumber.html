cool as a cucumber

IdiomLevel B2CasualFeelings & reactions

Very calm, even in a stressful situation.

The pilot was cool as a cucumber, even when the plane shook.

Tip: Use it after a form of 'be' or 'stay' (she was cool as a cucumber), and remember it describes calm in a tense moment, not being cold or unfriendly.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow did Amira's presentation go? I heard the projector died halfway through.
KenIt did! The whole screen went black in front of the clients.
OlenaOh no. Did she panic?
KenNot at all. She was cool as a cucumber. She just kept talking and used the whiteboard.
OlenaWow, I'd have frozen on the spot.
KenMe too. The clients were really impressed.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about someone you know who stays calm under pressure. What do they do?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

My older sister is a nurse, and she's always cool as a cucumber when things get busy. Last winter the ward was short-staffed and several patients needed help at once, but she just made a list and dealt with everything one step at a time. I really admire that, because I tend to get nervous when there's a lot going on.

Answer key: cool as a cucumber

  1. 1. B. Calm and relaxed even though something went wrong
  2. 2. cool as a cucumber
  3. 3. A. Yes, he was cool as a cucumber. He just walked out and helped people find the exit.
  4. 4. Example: My older sister is a nurse, and she's always cool as a cucumber when things get busy. Last winter the ward was short-staffed and several patients needed help at once, but she just made a list and dealt with everything one step at a time.
