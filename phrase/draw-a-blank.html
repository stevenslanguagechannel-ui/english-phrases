draw a blank

IdiomLevel B2CasualWork & study

To be unable to remember something when you need it.

I studied all night, but I drew a blank on the last question.

Tip: Use 'draw a blank' (past: 'drew a blank') for a sudden memory failure, often followed by 'on' + the thing you can't remember; it's about something you normally know, not something you never learned.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow did the meeting with the new client go?
KofiMostly fine, but I was so nervous that I drew a blank when she asked for the budget figures.
OlenaOh no. Did you tell her you'd send them later?
KofiYes, I just said I'd email her after lunch.
OlenaThat's smart. Nobody minds if you follow up properly.
