make it work

ExpressionLevel B1NeutralProblems & solutions

To find a way to deal with a difficult situation and succeed anyway.

The room is small, but we'll make it work.

Tip: Use it after a 'but' or 'even though' clause to say you'll succeed despite a limitation; it's often said as 'we'll make it work' or 'I'll make it work', and it works in formal speech too.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid the hotel confirm the extra bed for Kofi?
DiegoNo, they only have one room left, and it's tiny.
AmiraOh no. Should we look for somewhere else?
DiegoIt's the last weekend before the conference, and everything nearby is full.
AmiraOkay, we'll take turns on the sofa. We can make it work.
DiegoFine by me. I'll tell them we're taking the room.

Tell me about a time when you had to deal with a difficult situation with limited time or resources. What did you do?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I had to organise a small family dinner in a tiny flat, and we had far more guests than chairs. I borrowed some folding chairs from my neighbours and used the table in a different way, so we could make it work. It was a bit crowded, but everyone had a great evening.
