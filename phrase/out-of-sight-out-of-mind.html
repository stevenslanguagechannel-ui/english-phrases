out of sight, out of mind

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

If you don't see something, you stop thinking about it.

I hid my phone while studying. Out of sight, out of mind!

Tip: Say it as a complete comment after something happens, often with a slightly humorous or resigned tone; it is a fixed phrase, so don't change the word order.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid you finish the gym membership cancellation yet?
KofiNo, I keep forgetting. I only remember when I'm charged.
OlenaWhy don't you put the card in your bag instead of the kitchen drawer?
KofiBecause I never look in my bag. The card is in the drawer, but it's out of sight, out of mind.
OlenaThen set a reminder on your phone. Do it today!
KofiGood idea. I'll do it right now.
