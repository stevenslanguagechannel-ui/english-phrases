turn down

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To say no to an offer or invitation.

I had to turn down his invitation because I was sick.

Tip: Use it with an offer, invitation or request as the object: 'turn down an offer' or 'turn it down' (never 'turn down it'); it's polite enough for work, too.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraDid you hear back about the job in Lisbon?
KenYes, they offered me the position last week.
AmiraThat's great! So when do you start?
KenI don't. I turned it down in the end. The salary was too low, and I didn't want to move away from my family.
AmiraWow, that must have been a hard decision.
KenIt was, but I feel good about it now.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

A few months ago, a friend invited me to a weekend trip, but I had a big deadline at work, so I had to turn it down. I felt a bit guilty, but she understood. We went together the following month instead.
