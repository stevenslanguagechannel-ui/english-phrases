get caught up in

Phrasal verbLevel B2

To get caught up in something is to become so busy with it that you forget other things.

I got caught up in a TV show and forgot to cook dinner.

'Get caught up in' + noun or -ing means you are so busy with it you lose track of time.

Your turn
