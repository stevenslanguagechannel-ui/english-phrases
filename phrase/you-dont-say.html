You don't say!

ExpressionLevel B2CasualFeelings & reactions

A playful or sarcastic reply to something obvious. It can also show real surprise, depending on the tone.

A: It's really hot today. B: You don't say! I'm sweating through my shirt.

Tip: Said on its own, usually with a flat or exaggerated tone, it teases someone for stating the obvious; with rising intonation and a surprised face, it can show real surprise, so tone and context decide the meaning.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaI just checked the forecast. Apparently it rains a lot in Manchester.
KofiYou don't say! I've been wearing a raincoat since Monday.
OlenaOkay, fair. I'm still packing my umbrella, though.
KofiGood plan. Get a small one that fits in your bag.
OlenaAlready have one. It's bright yellow.

2. Fill the gap

3. Choose the best reply

Amira: The meeting room is freezing again. I think the air conditioning is on too high.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time someone told you something that was completely obvious. How did you react?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last week my flatmate told me the shop on our street closes early on Sundays. I'd just walked there and found it shut, so I said, "You don't say!" with a big smile. We both laughed, and then we went to a café instead.

Answer key: You don't say!

  1. 1. D. Kofi is gently joking because Olena has told him something he already knows.
  2. 2. You don't say!
  3. 3. C. You don't say! I've been wearing my jacket all morning.
  4. 4. Example: Last week my flatmate told me the shop on our street closes early on Sundays. I'd just walked there and found it shut, so I said, "You don't say!" with a big smile. We both laughed, and then we went to a café instead.
