hear you out

Phrasal verbLevel B2

To listen to everything someone says before you judge or decide.

I don't love your idea, but I'll hear you out before I decide.

'Hear someone out' means to listen to all of their idea. Often you say 'Hear me out' before a surprising suggestion.
