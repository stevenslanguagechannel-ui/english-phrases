make the bed

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To pull the sheets and blanket flat and neat after you sleep.

I always make the bed before I leave the house.

Tip: Use 'make the bed' (not 'do the bed') in both British and American English; it works in any tense, e.g. 'I made the bed', 'Have you made the bed?'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraAre you ready? The taxi to the airport will be here in ten minutes.
KenAlmost. I just need to make the bed and take the rubbish out.
AmiraReally? We're going away for two weeks.
KenI know, but I hate coming home to a messy room.
AmiraFair enough. I'll check the windows while you do that.

Show an example answer

I get up around seven, have a quick shower and then make the bed while the coffee is brewing. It only takes a minute, but the room feels much calmer when I come home in the evening.

Answer key: make the bed

  1. 1. B. Pull the sheets and blanket flat and neat so the bed looks tidy
  2. 2. makes the bed
  3. 3. B. Not really, I just made the bed and put my clothes away.
  4. 4. Example: I get up around seven, have a quick shower and then make the bed while the coffee is brewing. It only takes a minute, but the room feels much calmer when I come home in the evening.
