speaking of

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

A way to start a new comment that connects to the thing someone just mentioned.

Speaking of coffee, have you tried the new café on Main Street?

Tip: Use 'Speaking of + noun' (or 'speaking of which') at the start of a sentence to link a new comment to something just mentioned; don't use it when the new topic has no real connection.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraI'm so tired. The train was late again this morning.
KofiAgain? That's the third time this week.
AmiraI know. I almost missed my first meeting.
KofiSpeaking of meetings, did you see the schedule for Friday's team lunch?
AmiraNo, I didn't. Is it at the usual place?
KofiNo, it's at the new Thai restaurant near the station.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a place you would like to visit in the future.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I'd really like to visit Japan one day, mostly because I love the food and the public transport. Speaking of food, I'd definitely try to eat at small local places instead of big tourist restaurants. I think that's the best way to understand a country.

Answer key: speaking of

  1. 1. C. It introduces a new comment connected to something just mentioned.
  2. 2. Speaking of
  3. 3. A. Speaking of banks, I need to stop by one and change some money before my trip.
  4. 4. Example: I'd really like to visit Japan one day, mostly because I love the food and the public transport. Speaking of food, I'd definitely try to eat at small local places instead of big tourist restaurants. I think that's the best way to understand a country.
