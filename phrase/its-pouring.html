it's pouring

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

It is raining very hard.

Take an umbrella. It's pouring outside!

Tip: Use it's pouring (or it's pouring down) about the weather right now; for a general habit, say it pours or it poured.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraAre you still coming to the market this morning?
KofiI was going to, but look out of the window. It's pouring.
AmiraOh no. I thought it was just a light shower.
KofiIt started ten minutes ago and it's getting worse.
AmiraLet's wait a bit. Maybe it'll stop by eleven.
KofiFine, I'll make some tea while we wait.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "it's pouring" mean?

2. Fill the gap

I can't hear you very well because and the rain is so loud on the roof.

3. Choose the best reply

Olena: Did you walk to the station this morning?

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time bad weather changed your plans.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last summer my friends and I planned a picnic in the park. When we arrived, it was pouring, so we ran to a nearby cafe instead. It turned out to be a fun day anyway.

Answer key: it's pouring

  1. 1. C. It is raining very hard.
  2. 2. it's pouring
  3. 3. C. No, I took a taxi. It was pouring.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer my friends and I planned a picnic in the park. When we arrived, it was pouring, so we ran to a nearby cafe instead. It turned out to be a fun day anyway.
