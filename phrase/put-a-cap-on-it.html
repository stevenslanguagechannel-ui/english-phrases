put a cap on it

IdiomLevel B2NeutralTime & effort

To set a maximum limit on how much you do or use something.

I watch too much TV, so I'm putting a cap on it: one hour a night.

Tip: Use 'put a cap on' + the thing you're limiting (put a cap on spending, on overtime); the cap can be a number or an amount, and it's often your own decision or a rule someone sets.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid you see how much we spent on takeaway last month?
KofiDon't remind me. It was almost three hundred.
OlenaThat's too much for two people. We need to put a cap on it.
KofiFair. How about eighty a month, and we cook the rest?
OlenaEighty works. I'll set a reminder on my phone.
