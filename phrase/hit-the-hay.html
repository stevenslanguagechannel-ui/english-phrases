hit the hay

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To go to bed.

I'm so tired. I'm going to hit the hay.

Tip: Use it as a verb phrase ('I'm going to hit the hay', 'I should hit the hay'); it's friendly and informal, so keep it for chats with friends and colleagues rather than formal writing.

Hear it in conversation

KenAre you still working on the slides? It's nearly midnight.
AmiraJust finishing the last one. My eyes are going funny.
KenSend me what you have. I'll check it in the morning.
AmiraThanks, Ken. I'll hit the hay as soon as I press send.
KenGood idea. You'll do a better job with some sleep.

4. Your turn: speak

Do you usually stay up late, or do you go to bed early? Tell me about your evening routine.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I'm quite an early person, so I usually hit the hay around ten thirty. I like to read for a bit first, because it helps me switch off. On weekends I stay up a little later, but not much.

Answer key: hit the hay

  1. 1. B. Go to bed
  2. 2. hit the hay
  3. 3. C. Yes, I'm going to hit the hay as soon as I get home.
  4. 4. Example: I'm quite an early person, so I usually hit the hay around ten thirty. I like to read for a bit first, because it helps me switch off. On weekends I stay up a little later, but not much.
