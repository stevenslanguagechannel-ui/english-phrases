break a sweat

IdiomLevel B1CasualEveryday life

To start sweating because you are exercising.

I only walked slowly, so I didn't even break a sweat.

Tip: Usually used in the negative ('didn't even break a sweat') to say something was easy or not tiring; it is about physical effort, not stress or nerves.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHow was the new gym class? You look really fresh.
DiegoHonestly, I was a bit disappointed. It was meant to be a hard workout.
OlenaReally? Was it too slow?
DiegoYes, we just stretched and did some gentle exercises. I hardly broke a sweat.
OlenaMaybe try the evening class next time. People say it's much tougher.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a type of exercise or physical activity you do, or would like to try. How hard is it for you?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I go swimming twice a week, and at first it was really tiring. Now I can do thirty lengths without breaking a sweat, so I'm thinking about joining a running group to push myself a bit more.

Answer key: break a sweat

  1. 1. B. He barely got hot or tired from the exercise
  2. 2. break a sweat
  3. 3. C. Not at all, I barely broke a sweat. They were lighter than they looked.
  4. 4. Example: I go swimming twice a week, and at first it was really tiring. Now I can do thirty lengths without breaking a sweat, so I'm thinking about joining a running group to push myself a bit more.
