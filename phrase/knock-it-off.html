knock it off

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

Stop doing something that annoys you.

Knock it off! I'm trying to work.

Tip: Use it as a command, often with an exclamation mark, to people you know well; it sounds rude to a boss or a stranger, so avoid it in formal situations.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaKofi, why do you keep tapping the table? It's been ten minutes.
KofiSorry, I didn't even notice I was doing it.
OlenaPlease knock it off. I'm trying to finish this report before the meeting.
KofiOkay, okay. I'll go and get some air instead.
OlenaThanks. I'll be done in half an hour.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a habit that other people do and that you find annoying. What do you do when it happens?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

I really can't stand it when people chew loudly next to me, especially on public transport. If it's a friend, I just say, 'Hey, knock it off!' and we laugh about it. With strangers, I usually just move to another seat.

Answer key: knock it off

  1. 1. C. Stop doing something that is annoying me
  2. 2. knock it off
  3. 3. A. Oh, sorry! I'll knock it off.
  4. 4. Example: I really can't stand it when people chew loudly next to me, especially on public transport. If it's a friend, I just say, 'Hey, knock it off!' and we laugh about it. With strangers, I usually just move to another seat.
