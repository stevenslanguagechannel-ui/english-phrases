run it by someone

ExpressionLevel B2NeutralWork & study

To tell someone your idea or plan to get their opinion before you decide.

Let me run this idea by my manager first.

Tip: Use the pattern 'run + idea/plan/draft + by + person' (or 'run it by someone'), and remember the other person's opinion matters but you usually make the final choice.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaI'm thinking of moving my study group to Sunday evenings.
KofiHave you told everyone yet?
OlenaNot yet. I'd like to run it by Amira first, since she's the one who books the room.
KofiGood idea. She might know about a clash.
OlenaExactly. If she's fine with it, I'll send the message tonight.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you asked someone for their opinion before making an important decision.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I was thinking about changing my course, so I ran the idea by an older cousin who had done something similar. She pointed out a few problems I hadn't considered, like the cost and the workload. In the end I stayed on my course, and I'm glad I got her opinion first.

Answer key: run it by someone

  1. 1. C. Tell Amira the plan to hear her opinion before deciding
  2. 2. run it by
  3. 3. A. Why not run it by Diego before you submit it? He's good with numbers.
