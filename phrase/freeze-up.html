freeze up

Phrasal verbLevel B1CasualProblems & solutions

When a computer or phone freezes up, the screen stops moving and nothing works.

My laptop always freezes up when I open too many tabs.

Tip: Use it for devices and programs that stop responding; it's intransitive, so say 'my phone froze up', not 'it froze up my phone'.

Hear it in conversation

AmiraAre you still there, Kofi? Your face has gone completely still.
KofiSorry! My video call just froze up. Can you hear me now?
AmiraYes, now I can. Was it your Wi-Fi?
KofiMaybe. I'll switch to my phone's hotspot and rejoin.
AmiraGood idea. I'll wait here.

Workbook

1. What does it mean?

In this conversation, what does "froze up" mean?

2. Fill the gap

The app keeps every time I try to upload a photo, so I'll restart my phone.

3. Choose the best reply

Ken: Sorry, I missed your last sentence. My screen just stopped moving.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time technology let you down at a bad moment. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month I was giving an online presentation when my laptop froze up in the middle of my second slide. I had to restart it while everyone waited, which felt awful. Now I always keep a backup copy on my phone.

Answer key: freeze up

  1. 1. A. The call stopped working and the picture stopped moving
  2. 2. freezing up
  3. 3. C. No problem! Did your laptop freeze up again?
  4. 4. Example: Last month I was giving an online presentation when my laptop froze up in the middle of my second slide. I had to restart it while everyone waited, which felt awful. Now I always keep a backup copy on my phone.
