it's on me

ExpressionLevel B1CasualProblems & solutions

It is my fault or my responsibility.

We missed the train. It's on me, I left the house too late.

Tip: Use it when you accept blame or take responsibility, often right after something goes wrong: 'Sorry, that's on me.' Careful: 'it's on me' can also mean 'I'm paying' (see 'the next round is on me'), so context matters.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaHi Kofi, did you get the email from the client? They say the files never arrived.
KofiOh no. I think I attached the wrong folder yesterday.
OlenaReally? They need them before the meeting at three.
KofiI'm sorry, it's on me. I should have checked before I hit send.
OlenaOkay, thanks for being honest. Can you send the right files now?
KofiDoing it right now, and I'll call them to explain.

Show an example answer

Last year I forgot to book a hotel for a weekend trip with friends, and everything was full when I finally checked. I told them straight away, "It's on me, I should have done it weeks ago." We ended up staying in a small town nearby, and it actually turned out fine.

Answer key: it's on me

  1. 1. A. It was my mistake and I take responsibility.
  2. 2. it's on me
  3. 3. C. Yes, it's on me. I should have left earlier.
  4. 4. Example: Last year I forgot to book a hotel for a weekend trip with friends, and everything was full when I finally checked. I told them straight away, "It's on me, I should have done it weeks ago." We ended up staying in a small town nearby, and it actually turned out fine.
