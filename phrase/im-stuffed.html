I'm stuffed

ExpressionLevel B1CasualEveryday life

I have eaten so much that I can't eat any more.

Thanks, but no more food for me. I'm stuffed.

Tip: Use 'I'm stuffed' after a meal to say you are completely full, often to politely refuse more food; it is casual, so don't use it for being full of something other than food.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaThere's still some pasta left. Do you want another plate?
KofiIt was delicious, but I'm stuffed. I couldn't manage another bite.
OlenaNot even a little bit of garlic bread?
KofiMaybe later. I need to sit for a while first.
OlenaFine, I'll put the rest in a container for you to take home.
KofiPerfect, thank you. That will be my lunch tomorrow.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you ate too much. What happened?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

At my cousin's wedding last summer, there was so much food that I kept going back for more. By the end of the main course I was stuffed, but then they brought out a huge cake, so I had to say no politely. I felt sleepy for the rest of the evening!

Answer key: I'm stuffed

  1. 1. A. I have eaten so much that I can't eat any more.
  2. 2. stuffed
  3. 3. B. Thanks, but I'm stuffed. Maybe I'll have some later.
  4. 4. Example: At my cousin's wedding last summer, there was so much food that I kept going back for more. By the end of the main course I was stuffed, but then they brought out a huge cake, so I had to say no politely. I felt sleepy for the rest of the evening!
