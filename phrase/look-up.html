look up

Phrasal verbLevel B1NeutralEveryday life

To look up information is to search for it in a dictionary or online.

I didn't know the word, so I looked it up online.

Tip: Use 'look something up' or 'look up something'; with a pronoun it must split: 'look it up', never 'look up it'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDo you know what 'ubiquitous' means? It's in this article.
KofiNot exactly. I think it's something like 'everywhere'.
OlenaI'd like to be sure before I write it in my notes.
KofiLet me look it up on my phone.
KofiOkay, it means 'seeming to be present everywhere'. You were close.

Show an example answer

Answer key: look up

  1. 1. A. Search for the meaning in a reliable source
  2. 2. look it up
  3. 3. C. Not sure. I'll look it up on the app.
