in a pinch

IdiomLevel B2NeutralProblems & solutions

In a difficult moment when you have no better option.

We can use a spoon as a screwdriver in a pinch.

Tip: Use it at the end of a sentence or clause to say something works as a backup: 'X will do in a pinch'. It suggests the solution is not ideal.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaOur train was cancelled, and the next one isn't until midnight. What should we do?
KofiI could ask my cousin to drive us. He lives about twenty minutes away.
OlenaWould he mind? It's quite late.
KofiHe won't. He always says I can call him in a pinch.
OlenaOkay, let's wait ten minutes and see if another train appears first.
KofiGood idea. If not, I'll call him.

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time when you had to use something or someone as a temporary solution.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last month my laptop charger stopped working just before an online meeting. I borrowed my brother's phone charger and used it to keep going. It's not ideal, but it works in a pinch.
