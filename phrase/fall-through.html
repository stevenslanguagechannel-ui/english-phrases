fall through

Phrasal verbLevel B2NeutralProblems & solutions

When a plan does not happen, it fails or is cancelled.

Our beach plans fell through because of the rain.

Tip: Use 'fall through' for plans, deals or arrangements that collapse; it has no object, so say 'The deal fell through', not 'The rain fell through the deal'.

Hear it in conversation

OlenaDid you ever get that flat near the station?
KofiNo, it fell through. The landlord changed his mind at the last minute.
OlenaOh no, after all that paperwork?
KofiI know. I'm viewing another place on Saturday, though.
OlenaGood. Hopefully that one works out.

Tell me about a time when a plan you had made did not work out.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last summer I planned a weekend camping trip with some friends, but it fell through at the last minute because two of them got ill. We were disappointed, so we had a picnic in a local park instead, which was actually really nice.

Answer key: fall through

  1. 1. D. The arrangement failed to happen
  2. 2. fell through
  3. 3. A. No, it fell through when her flight was cancelled.
  4. 4. Example: Last summer I planned a weekend camping trip with some friends, but it fell through at the last minute because two of them got ill. We were disappointed, so we had a picnic in a local park instead, which was actually really nice.
