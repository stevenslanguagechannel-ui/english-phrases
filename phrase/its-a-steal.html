it's a steal

IdiomLevel B2CasualEveryday life

Something is so cheap that it feels like you got it almost for free.

Only twenty euros for this lamp? It's a steal!

Tip: Say 'It's a steal' (or 'That's a steal') about something you buy or find at a surprisingly low price; it is a positive comment, so don't use it for something cheap and poor quality.

Hear it in conversation

KenDid you ever find a desk for your new flat?
OlenaYes! I got a solid wood one from a online sale this morning.
KenNice. How much was it?
OlenaSixty pounds, and it was originally almost four hundred. It's a steal.
KenWow, send me the link before it sells out!

3. Choose the best reply

4. Your turn: speak

Tell me about something you bought that was a really good deal. What was it, and why were you so pleased?

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Last year I bought a second-hand bicycle from a neighbour for about eighty euros. It was almost new and would have cost four times that in a shop, so it was a steal. I still use it to get to work every day.

Answer key: it's a steal

  1. 1. A. The price is so low that it feels like a fantastic bargain.
  2. 2. a steal
  3. 3. B. Fifteen euros? That's a steal!
  4. 4. Example: Last year I bought a second-hand bicycle from a neighbour for about eighty euros. It was almost new and would have cost four times that in a shop, so it was a steal. I still use it to get to work every day.
