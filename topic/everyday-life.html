back on your feet

IdiomLevel B2Neutral

Healthy and active again after being ill or having problems.

Tip: Use it after 'be' or 'get' (be back on your feet, get back on your feet) for people recovering from illness, injury or a hard period; it can also describe a business or a person's finances recovering.

AmiraIs Kofi still off work? I haven't seen him since the flu hit the office.
DiegoHe came in on Monday, but only for half a day. He looked pretty pale.
AmiraPoor guy. He was in bed for almost two weeks, wasn't he?
DiegoYes, but he says he's nearly back on your feet... sorry, back on his feet. He even went for a short walk yesterday.
AmiraGood. I'll bring him some soup tomorrow so he doesn't have to cook.
