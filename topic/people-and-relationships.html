bang on about

Phrasal verbLevel B2Casual

To talk about the same subject for too long, in a boring or annoying way.

Tip: Use it with 'about' plus the subject (bang on about politics, bang on about work); it's mildly critical, so say it about other people more than to their face. It's also common in the form 'go banging on about'.

AmiraHow was the train ride with Kofi? Six hours is a long time.
KenHonestly, I lost the will to live around hour two.
AmiraWhy, what happened?
KenHe wouldn't stop banging on about his new running app. Steps, routes, heart rate, everything.
AmiraHa, I'd have put my headphones on straight away.
KenI did, eventually. He didn't even notice.

claim to fame

IdiomLevel B2Casual

The one interesting or impressive thing a person or place is known for.

Tip: Use it with 'my/his/her/its claim to fame is...' or 'X's claim to fame is that...'; it's usually light and a bit playful, so don't use it for serious achievements.

OlenaSo what's special about your hometown, Kofi?
KofiHonestly, not much. Our only claim to fame is a giant statue of a pineapple.
OlenaA pineapple? Seriously?
KofiSeriously. People drive for hours just to take a photo next to it.
OlenaOkay, now I want to visit.

make it up to you

ExpressionLevel B2Neutral

To do something nice to fix a problem you caused.

Tip: Use the pattern 'make it up to someone' (often followed by 'with' or 'by'); the 'it' stays in the phrase, so don't say 'make up to you'.

OlenaKofi, I'm so sorry I forgot to pick you up from the airport. You had to take a taxi.
KofiHonestly, it was fine. I just wish you'd texted me.
OlenaI know, my phone died and I lost track of time. Let me make it up to you. Dinner on Friday, my treat?
KofiYou don't have to do that, but I won't say no to free food.
OlenaGreat. Pick any place you like.

Show an example answer

meet halfway

Phrasal verbLevel B1Neutral

To reach a compromise where each side gives a little.

Tip: Use it as 'meet someone halfway (on something)' or 'meet halfway'; both sides give a little, so it often follows a disagreement about plans, money or time.

OlenaKofi, I really need the report by Thursday, but you said Monday.
KofiThursday is tough. I'm still waiting on the sales figures.
OlenaCould we meet halfway? How about Friday morning?
KofiFriday morning works. I'll send you what I have on Thursday evening, too.
OlenaPerfect, that helps a lot. Thanks.

16. Your turn: speak

Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone and found a solution that worked for you both.

Answer out loud in 2–3 sentences and use the phrase. Then compare with the example answer.

Show an example answer

Answer key

  1. bang on about
    1. C. Talking about something for so long that it becomes boring
    2. banging on about
    3. C. It was fine, but one guy banged on about his holiday photos all evening.
    4. Example: My uncle is a good example. Whenever we meet, he bangs on about his garden for at least half an hour, and I just nod and smile. He's a lovely person, but I've learned to change the subject quickly.
  2. claim to fame
    5. A. The one interesting thing the town is known for
    6. claim to fame
    7. C. Not really. Our one claim to fame is a bakery that's open at 4 a.m.
    8. Example: My hometown's main claim to fame is its night market, which is popular with visitors from all over the region. Other than that, it's a pretty quiet place. My cousin also has a small claim to fame, because he once appeared on local TV for finding a lost dog.
  3. make it up to you
    9. B. Do something nice to repair a mistake I made
    10. make it up to you
    11. C. I know, and I'm sorry. I'll make it up to you, I promise.
    12. Example: Last year I forgot my friend's birthday because I was busy with exams. I felt terrible, so I called her straight away and apologised. Then I took her out for her favourite lunch to make it up to her.
  4. meet halfway
    13. C. Agree on a middle option that is fair to both people
    14. meet halfway
    15. C. Let's meet halfway and say £450.
    16. Example: Last year my flatmate and I disagreed about how often to clean the kitchen. I wanted to do it daily and she preferred once a week, so we met halfway and agreed on every three days. It worked well, and we both felt the other person had listened.
